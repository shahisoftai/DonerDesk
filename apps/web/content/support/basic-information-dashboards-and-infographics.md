# Dashboards and Infographics

A dashboard shows your project's figures live. An infographic is the same page published as a picture or document to share. You build both in the same place: **Dashboards and infographics** in the left menu.

## Start a new one

1. Open **Dashboards and infographics** and choose **New**. The page is called **New dashboard or infographic**.
2. Under **Start from**, choose one of eight standard starters (for example **Response dashboard**, **Flash update**, **Country brief**) or **A blank page**. Another 150 or so are under **More templates**; type in the search box to narrow them.
3. Give it a **Name**, say **Who is it for** (**Only me**, **Everyone on one project**, **The whole organisation**) and choose the **Project**.
4. Choose a **Page size** and the **Look of the blocks** (the default is **Flat title bar**). You can change both later.
5. Choose **Make it**.

A starter asks you to choose an indicator for each role (for example "The headline indicator"). You do not have to: a role you leave empty is filled with the project's next unused indicator, and every block can be changed afterwards. **A blank page** starts empty, with all of the project's data listed beside it.

## Add things to the page

1. Choose **Edit**. The **Add** panel opens on the side.
2. Under **Your project's data** you see every indicator of the project. Use **Search indicators** to find one.
3. For one indicator, choose **Show as a number** (#) or **Show as a chart**. To make something from several, tick them and choose **Chart of N**, **Compare latest values** or **Table**.
4. For anything else, choose a block from the list in **Add**, or drag it onto the page. A starter set adds several blocks at once.
5. **Upload a picture** adds a picture block (see below).
6. Choose **Save**. **Undo** and **Redo** work on every change.

## Place and size anything, anywhere

A new page is a free page: every block goes exactly where you put it and can be any size.

- **Drag** a block to move it. Drag a handle on any edge or corner to resize it (hold **Shift** to keep its shape). Drag the round handle above it to turn it.
- Select a block and open the **Build** tab of the **Edit** panel: **Position and size** has **X**, **Y**, **Width**, **Height** and **Turn°** in pixels, and **To front**, **Forward**, **Backward**, **To back**.
- The arrow keys move the selected block (hold **Shift** to move further). Select several blocks to align or space them out.
- Blocks guide themselves to the page edge and to each other while you drag; untick **Snap to margins and other blocks** to turn that off.
- **Page size** (in the **About this canvas** box while editing) offers screen sizes, A4, A3, letter, slide and social-media sizes. A page grows to hold everything you place on it.

## Charts: every kind, and the numbers on them

Select a chart, then on the **Build** tab choose **Kind of chart**. Every kind is listed. If the current data cannot be drawn as that kind, the chart switches to data that can and tells you in one sentence (for example "Pie needs each indicator's latest value, so the chart now shows that"). A kind that would mislead is refused with the reason: a pie cannot show a rate (a percentage or ratio), because slices must add up.

Under **Numbers on the chart**:

- **Show the numbers** puts the figures on the chart.
- **Show** chooses **The figure**, **Its share**, **The name** or **Name and figure**.
- **Written as** chooses **Full**, **Short** (12.3K) or one or two decimals.
- **Where** chooses **Above**, **Below**, **Left**, **Right**, **Inside**, **Outside** or **Automatic**, and **Size (px)** sets the size.

Numbers can be shown on bars, lines, areas, pies, histograms, scatter charts, waterfalls, treemaps, maps and more. Gauges, progress bars and rings write their own figure, and a box plot has no single figure to write, so those ignore the setting.

## Your own words, fonts and sizes

Select a block and open the **Format** tab.

- **Words**: type your own **Headline**, **Subtitle**, **Caption**, **Source line** or **As-of line**. Leave a box empty to use the automatic words. Words you typed show "Edited: these words no longer follow the data." The **Design check** warns when typed words on a figure block state a number, because it will not change when the data does. Text always wraps; it is never cut short.
- **Text**: choose the **Font**, **Size (px)**, **Weight**, **Italic**, **Letter case**, **Letter spacing (px)**, **Line height**, **Alignment** and **Colour** of the block's text. Any size is allowed.
- **Title**: size, weight, colour and alignment of the heading.

## Themes reach every block

In **About this canvas** choose a **Look** (and a **Tone** where the look has them). Every block takes the look's fonts and colours, in the page and in downloaded files. If a block has colours of its own, the panel says "N blocks differ from the theme": open it to see which, and choose **Use the theme** for one block or **Reset all to the theme**. Sizes, words and positions you set are kept. **Load a theme file** and **Save this theme as a file** work with your own look.

## Pictures, logos and icons

Choose **Upload a picture** in **Add**, or drag a picture file onto the page. PNG, JPEG, WebP and SVG are accepted, up to 6 MB. The picture is kept in your organisation's own Google Drive (in a folder called 07-Visual-Media), so **Google Drive must be connected** (Settings → Storage); if it is not, the upload says so and nothing is saved elsewhere. An SVG that contains script or links to other files is refused, and an accepted SVG is turned into a plain picture. Give the picture a description in the block's **Description for people who cannot see it**; a page you share needs one. Pictures show to people who open a share link too, but only the pictures that page uses.

## Share, publish and delete

- **Share and publish** (under the page) makes a link, a kept copy, a PDF or a picture. A page for a donor must pass the **Design check** first.
- Downloads (PNG, PDF, PPTX) are laid out in one column in reading order, so a page with blocks placed freely is approximated in a file: check the file before you send it.
- To remove one, open it, choose **More**, then **Delete**, and confirm. This cannot be undone.

See also: **The Sector Library** (starting dashboards for each sector), **Chart Studio** (under **Tools**) for building and saving one chart on its own, and **Maps**.
