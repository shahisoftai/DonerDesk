# Roles and Permissions in Depth

The seven workspace roles and the full capability matrix are in [User roles and permissions](/support/getting-started/user-roles-and-permissions). This page covers how roles interact with the rest of DonorDesk.

## Workspace role vs project assignment

- Your **workspace role** (set on the Team page by an Admin) defines what you can do.
- Your **project assignments** (project → **Settings → Team**) define which projects you work on and your **project role** there.

## Seats

Seats are counted by role. Full seats are every role except **Viewer**; Viewers have their own, larger allowance (Starter 2, Team and above unlimited). Give board members, donors and auditors the Viewer role.

## Sensitive actions and who can take them

| Action | Who |
|---|---|
| Plan and schedule activities | Admin, Project Manager, M&E Officer |
| Update the activities you lead (status, notes, place) | Admin, Project Manager, M&E Officer, Grants Officer, Field Officer, Compliance Officer |
| Show or hide work planning for a project | Admin |
| Require two-step sign-in for everyone; create API keys and webhooks | Admin |
| Approve a report | Admin, Project Manager |
| Decide on flagged statements | Admin, Project Manager |
| Confirm sharing of confidential (Sensitive / Highly sensitive) evidence in an export | Admin, Grants Officer |
| Accept a high-severity compliance risk | Users with checklist permission, confirming authority |
| Delete evidence | Admin |
| Manage billing, team and settings | Admin |
| Manage AI Writing Style | Admin, Project Manager |
| View the audit log | Admin, Project Manager, Compliance Officer |

## What a Field Officer is shown

A Field Officer holds neither the **money view** nor the **analysis view** that every other role has: the finance register, budget, funding and billing summary, and the data sheet, management report, portfolio, framework history and changes of plan are closed to them, in the app and in the data it asks for. They see only the people they registered. Their pages are Home, Inbox, Projects and, inside a project, the work plan and the forms. See [How to do your field work on your phone](/support/how-to/do-your-field-work-on-your-phone).

## Separation of duties

A common setup is: Field Officers fill in forms, log activities and upload evidence; M&E Officers enter and verify indicators; Grants Officers draft and edit reports; Project Managers approve; Compliance Officers verify evidence and manage the checklist; Viewers export.

## Changing roles

Admins change roles on **Team**. The screen shows what the person will gain and lose. Changes are audited. See [How to change a role](/support/how-to/manage-team-roles-permissions).
