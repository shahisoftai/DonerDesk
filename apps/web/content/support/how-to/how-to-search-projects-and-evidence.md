# How to Search and Filter Projects and Evidence

DonorDesk has one search for the whole workspace, and filters on the lists where you work most.

## Search or jump to… (the command palette)

1. Press **Ctrl K** (Mac **⌘K**) anywhere, or click **Search or jump to…** at the top.
2. Type at least two letters. You see matching **projects** (by title, code, donor or country), matching **files** (by their title and text) and the **pages and actions** that fit what you typed, for example *inbox*, *create a project*, *invite a person*, *enter data*. Inside a project the list starts with that project's own pages.
3. Move with **↑ ↓** and press **Enter**, or click a row. **Esc** closes it.

You only see what your role may open: someone who cannot create projects is not offered **Create a project**, and a file you may not read is not listed. Press **?** (outside a text box) for the keyboard shortcuts.

## Projects

1. Click **Projects** in the left menu.
2. Type in the search box (title, code, donor or country).
3. Narrow with the **status**, **sector**, **donor** and **country** filters and change the sort (for example **Soonest deadline**), then press **Apply**.
4. Each row has a **⋯** menu with shortcuts (open, results framework, enter data, reports, project settings). Pointing at a title shows a small card with its code, donor, status and days left.

Filters are kept in the page address, so you can bookmark or share a filtered view.

## Evidence

On **Reports → Evidence** (all projects) or a project's **Evidence** tab use the filter bar:

- Search by **file name or title**
- **Verification** status (Uploaded, AI tagged, Pending review, Verified, Needs correction, Rejected, Archived)
- **Confidentiality** (Public, Internal, Sensitive, Highly sensitive)
- Reporting period

## Other lists

- **Inbox → To do** filters by project; its counts show what is late and what waits for review.
- **Audit log** filters by actor, event and entity ([guide](/support/how-to/use-the-audit-trail)).
- **Team** search by name or email and filter by role.
- **Inbox → Updates** filters All, Unread or Read.
