# How to Make an Infographic in French, Spanish or Arabic

1. Open the design and choose its **Language**: English, French, Spanish or Arabic.
2. Labels, numbers and dates follow that language. Arabic digits stay Latin.
3. Arabic reads right to left, but maps and charts are not mirrored, so geography is never flipped.

Text you typed is not translated; write it in the language you want. Translations of the product's own labels are drafts until a native reader reviews them.
