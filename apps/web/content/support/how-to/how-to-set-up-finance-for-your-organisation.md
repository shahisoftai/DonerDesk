# How to Set Up Finance for Your Organisation

Before project teams record money, an **Admin** sets the lists every project's finance uses: currencies, who may approve, funders, exchange rates and budget categories. You do this once, then adjust as needed.

Open **Settings → Finance**. (You need the **Admin** role.) Every section saves on its own.

## 1. Currencies

1. Under **Currencies**, choose the **Reporting currency**, the one your totals are shown in (for example USD).
2. Optional: choose a **Local currency**.
3. Click **Save currencies**.

## 2. Who may approve spending

Under **Who may approve**, there is one switch: **The person who entered an entry cannot approve it**.

- **Off** (default): anyone with approval rights (Admin, Project Manager) may approve an entry, including the person who entered it.
- **On**: a second person must approve every expense, amount received and milestone. Turn this on if your donor or auditor expects separation of duties.

## 3. Funding sources

**Funding sources** is the list of who funds your work. Projects pick from it when they add funding.

1. Under **Add funding source**, type the **Name**.
2. Choose how it counts:
   - **Counts in totals**: it is your own funding. Its amounts are added into a project's **Obligated** and **Received**.
   - **Listed only**: it is shown (for example a partner's cost-share) but never added in.
3. Click **Add funding source**.

Each existing source shows whether it **Counts in totals** or is **Listed only** (you can change this) and has a **Retire** button, which hides it from new choices. Entries already recorded against it stay as they are.

## 4. Exchange rates

If projects spend in more than one currency, add rates so totals can be shown in the reporting currency.

1. Under **Exchange rates**, click **Add rate**.
2. Fill in **From** and **To** (currency codes), the **units of the second currency for one of the first** (for example `1.0850`), the date it **Takes effect on**, and an optional **Source**.
3. Click **Add rate**.

Rules to know:
- A rate applies from its day until a later one is entered.
- **Rates are never edited.** To change one, add a new rate with a new date.
- A payment is converted with the rate in force **on the payment's day**, never a later one. With no rate, the total says **Rate needed** instead of guessing.

## 5. Budget categories

DonorDesk already has **staff and volunteers, travel, supplies, equipment, services and other**. Under **Budget categories**, add your own with a **Code** and **Name**, then click **Add category**.

## After setup

Project teams record money in each project's **Finance** tab. See [How to track money in the Finance tab](/support/how-to/use-the-finance-tab).
