# How to Do Your Field Work on Your Phone

This page is for **field officers**: the people who visit places, fill in forms, take photos and record what was done. DonorDesk shows a field officer only what the work needs, so the screens are short.

## Set up once, while you have signal

1. Open the link your manager sent and join with your own name and password. If you have an account already, sign in at `donordesk.online`.
2. On your phone, open `donordesk.online/field`. Tip: use **Add to Home screen** in your browser so it is one tap next time.
3. Under **Download for offline**, open the **Project** list, choose your project and choose **Download or refresh**. You should see that the form was downloaded and you can fill it with no connection.

The download holds the form questions, the places of your own work (your facilities and the areas above them) and your planned work. It holds no other people's records and no figures. It is removed when you sign out, so do not sign out while you are in the field. If your manager gives you more facilities or changes the form, choose **Download or refresh** again.

## Your Home screen

**Given to you** lists the facilities or places your manager gave you for a form, with the form's name, a due day and **To do** or **Started**. Started ones come first, then the earliest due day. **Tap a facility** and its form opens, ready to fill. Under it, **My week** shows your planned activities.

If the list says **All your facilities are done**, there is nothing more given to you. Ask your manager if more should be given to you.

## Fill in a form

1. Open the form (from **Given to you**, or **On this phone** on the field page). You see only the facilities given to you.
2. Choose **Start** next to the facility you are at.
3. Fill it in one section at a time. A **draft is saved while you fill**, on the phone when you have no signal, so you can stop and come back (**Continue**).
4. Take photos where a question asks, capture the GPS at the main gate, and have the signatures drawn on the screen.
5. **Send**. With no signal the button reads **Save on this phone**, and the form is sent by itself when the phone is online again. **Waiting to send** lists what has not gone yet.

If you cannot visit a facility, choose **Not visited** and write why. Your manager sees it.

## Record what you did, or a photo

Tap **Record**. You can choose **Something we did** (a session, visit or delivery), **A photo or file** (proof of what happened) or **A form** (a survey or checklist for this project).

## What you will not see

You do not see the project's money, results analysis, reports, settings, team or other people's records. Those pages show **Page not found**. That is on purpose: it keeps your phone simple and the project's information safe. If someone tries to open one of those pages by its address, the app refuses with "This is not part of your work." If you need something that is not there, ask your manager.

## If something is missing

- **No Download or refresh button**: the project list may still be loading, or the phone thinks it is offline. Connect, reload the page and wait for **Choose a project…**. If the project is not in the list, you may not have accepted your invitation yet, or you signed in to a different account.
- **No facility under Given to you**: nothing is assigned to you yet, or you downloaded before your manager gave you the facilities. Ask, then choose **Download or refresh** again.
- **The facility opens a list of facilities**: you can choose only the ones given to you; pick yours and **Start**.
- **Your email works in a different organisation**: one email address belongs to one organisation. Ask your manager to invite you with another address.
