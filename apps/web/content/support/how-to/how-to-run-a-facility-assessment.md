# How to Run a Facility Assessment (or Any Long Checklist)

Use a **form about a facility** when a team visits places and fills a long tool: a health facility assessment, a school or shelter checklist, a monitoring visit. Each visit is one record. The form can have sections, tables, rows that repeat (blocks, wards, staff), a score, and wording in other languages. Assessors can fill it with no connection and finish over several days.

## Make the form

1. Open **Projects → your project → Data → Forms** and choose **New form**.
2. Start from something that exists, if you can:
   - **Make a draft from this** uses a ready-made form. The health facility assessment is built from the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Health Foundation tool. It has **not yet been checked by a sector reviewer**, and its weights and bands are illustrative. Read it and change it before you use it.
   - **Make a form from your own tool**: choose a Word or Excel file. DonorDesk reads its headings, questions and tables and proposes a form, showing the lines it could not read. Nothing is saved until you open the proposal in the designer and save it.
3. In the designer, **Add a section**, **Add a table** (rows against columns, for a tick grid or a count per room) or **Add a repeating group** (the same questions for each block, ward or person). A question can be **not applicable**, can offer the last visit's answer, and can show **only when** an earlier answer says so.
4. Under **Assessment settings**, choose **A facility** for "What one record is about". You can add rules across answers (functional beds not above sanctioned beds) and a score.
5. **Save the draft**, read it, then **Publish**. After publishing the questions cannot change; close the form and make a new one if they must.

A place only counts as a facility once it is marked as one on the project's **Where we work** page.

## Give facilities to assessors

Open the form and choose the **Facilities** tab. Tick facilities, choose the assessor (an active member of the project who can submit forms) and a due day, then **Give**. They are told in the app. The table above shows how far each district has got. The **Facilities**, **Assessments** and totals tabs are for the people who manage or review the form; an assessor sees only **Fill in**.

## Fill it in

Assessors open the form from **Given to you** on their Home (or **Work offline** on a phone). An assessor who has facilities given sees **only those**, not every facility, and a tap on one opens it. A manager or reviewer, and a form nobody assigned, chooses from every facility. When everything given is done the page says so. Choose **Start**:

- A long form shows one section at a time with a contents list and how much of each is answered. Problems are named in words beside the question.
- Facts that rarely change are offered from the last visit and marked **From the last visit**. Check them.
- A **draft is saved while you fill**: on the server when you are online, on the phone when you are not. Come back later and **Continue**. A draft that nobody touches for 60 days is removed.
- Sign with a finger on the **Signature** question. A photo of a stamp can be attached to a photo question.
- If a facility cannot be visited, choose **Not visited** and say why.
- **Send** (or **Save on this phone** when offline). The record waits for review.

## Review and follow up

The **Assessments** tab lists each visit with its score and anything to look at (a visit that was very quick, a GPS point far from the facility, two visits in one month). Open one to see the score by section, the items that scored lowest, the comparison with the visit before and the answers. **Ask for a fix** makes a request for a person, **Raise a problem** adds it to the project. Download the assessment as PDF, Word or Excel (a right-to-left language needs Word or Excel).

## Good to know

- A missing answer is never counted as 0. A score says how many items it stands on and shows no band until enough were answered.
- Totals can feed indicators: count the assessments, the share rated **Ready**, or the average score, one current record per facility.
- Urdu and Pashto are available for the assessor's screens; other screens show English. Every translation is a draft.
