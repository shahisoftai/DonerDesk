# How to Add Tiles, Maps, Lists and Notes to a Dashboard

Open the design, click **Edit**, then add a block. Each block stores only its settings and reads the data when someone opens it.

## Kinds of tile
- **Indicator (KPI) tile:** the latest figure for one indicator, its share of target, a small trend and a short sentence. It states change against the previous period with a figure. It never says "behind": a part-way figure cannot be late against a final target. You can give it a colour and title.
- **Number tile:** a coloured number from the finance register (obligated, received, spent, still to receive) or the plan (activities total, in progress, done, late). Money is never added across currencies; nothing recorded shows **Not recorded yet**, never 0.
- **Map:** results by place (hover, click, drill, time slider) or activity pins coloured by delivery state.
- **Project list:** projects by status or sector, all, assigned to you or bookmarked.
- **Note and heading:** text. A line that starts with `# ` is a heading.
- **Report table:** a saved report.

## Change a tile
Use **Edit** on the tile to change its settings in place; its place and size stay. A chart's data and drawing are changed in the Chart Studio.

## Move and resize
Drag a block, or use the keyboard: arrows move, Shift plus arrows resize. Nothing is saved until you click **Save**.

## Groups, tabs and repeaters
A **group** holds blocks together. **Tabs** show one tab at a time. A **repeater** draws what it holds once per project you can see, or once per place the project names. A file opens groups and tabs; a repeater is screen-only.
