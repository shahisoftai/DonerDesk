# How to Use Comment Threads

> A comment starts a conversation. Replies sit under it, so the discussion stays together, and the thread can be resolved when it is settled.

## Start a thread

Open the item (a file, an indicator value, a report section, a problem, or a single statement in a report). Under **Comments**, write in **Add a comment** and select **Add comment**.

## Reply

Select **Reply** under the comment you want to answer, write your reply and select **Send reply**. Replies appear under the first comment, oldest first.

## Resolve or reopen

- **Resolve** closes the thread. DonorDesk shows who resolved it and when, for example "Resolved by User 1234abcd · 21 Oct 2026, 13:08".
- **Reopen** opens a resolved thread again. You cannot reply to a thread while it is resolved; reopen it first.

## Who is told

- The person you mention.
- The person who started the thread, when someone replies.
- For a file, a problem, an activity or an indicator, the people responsible for it.

You are not told about your own comments.

## Comments on a report statement

In a report's **Statements** tab, each statement has its own comments. Use them to discuss one sentence, for example to ask where a figure came from. The comments stay with the sentence's wording, so they are kept when the statement is checked again.

## Good to know

- Threads go one level deep: you reply to the first comment, not to a reply.
- Names are not shown yet: people appear as a short code, for example "User 1234abcd".
- Comments cannot be edited or deleted after they are posted. Post a correction as a reply.
