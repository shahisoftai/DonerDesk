# How to Invite an Outside Partner

An **outside partner** is someone from another organisation who works on one or more of your projects, for example an implementing partner that reports activities. They see only the projects you choose.

Only an **Admin** can invite people.

## Invite a partner

1. Open **Team** and select **Invite member**.
2. Type the partner's work email.
3. Tick **Outside partner**.
4. Choose the role: **Field officer** (they report activities and enter indicator values) or **Viewer** (they only read).
5. Tick every project they should see. You must choose at least one.
6. Select **Send invite** and share the link shown.

## What the partner can see

- The projects you chose, and their logframe, activities and reporting periods.
- Evidence and files that belong to those projects.
- The screens they need to report: adding activities, uploading evidence and entering indicator values.

A partner has a short menu: **Home**, **My projects** and **Security** in the left menu, and **Today**, **Waiting** and **More** in the bar on a phone. Their **Home** is a page called **Your projects**: each project you gave them, the date of its next report (in red when late) and buttons to **Enter values**, **Add evidence** and **Open project**. Their **Projects** list shows only the projects you chose. They cannot see your other projects, the team list, billing, the audit trail, exports, funding, or report approvals. A page outside their access says *"This page is not part of your account."*

## Remove access

On the **Team** page use **Deactivate** (they cannot sign in; **Reactivate** brings them back) or **Delete** (final). Their access ends at their next page load. See [How to invite team members](/support/how-to/invite-team-members#deactivate-or-delete-someone).

## Limits

- Partners are not set to expire automatically.
- Outside partners can turn on two-step sign-in like everyone else (they have a **Security** page), and it counts when you [require it for the whole organisation](/support/how-to/require-two-step-sign-in). Remove their access when the work ends.
