# How to Set Up Your Places List

Your organisation keeps one **list of places** where you work. When a project, activity or result names a place, DonorDesk can then read it at the places above it: a locality's figures count toward its state and its country, and maps can show it. Setting up the list once keeps place names spelled the same everywhere.

Open **Settings → Places**. You need the **Admin** role to change the list.

## Add all the places of a country at once

1. Under **Add a country's places**, choose a **Country** from the list.
2. Click **See what would be added**. You see the places that would be added before anything is saved. Places you already have are left alone.
3. Confirm. The list is filled from the built-in boundaries, so names and codes are spelled the way the maps expect.

## Add one place by hand

1. Under **Add a place**, type its **Name** the way people say it (for example *Rabak* or *Um Gargur camp*).
2. Under **What is it?**, choose **Country**, **State or province**, **District or locality**, **Sub-district** or **Community or site**.
3. Under **Inside**, choose the place it sits in, for example *Dolo Ado › Somali › Ethiopia*, or **Nothing (top of the list)** for a country.
4. Click **Add place**.

A place must sit inside a place of a higher level (a district inside a province, not the other way round), and two places with the same name cannot sit in the same parent.

## Facility types

**Facility types** is your own list of kinds of facility (for example health centre or school). A place marked as a facility can be given one of these types. See [How to analyse places on a map](/support/how-to/analyse-places-on-a-map).

## Places to confirm

Under **Places to confirm** you see words your team typed as a location that no place in your list answers to yet, with how often each was used and where (plan, completed activities). The most used come first.

1. For each real place, click **Add place** to add it properly.
2. Optionally click **Place them on the map, one by one** to pin them.

Nothing your team typed is changed. Their words stay as typed, and DonorDesk matches them to your list when it reads them.

## Import and export

Places can also be imported from a sheet and downloaded. See [How to download places and map results](/support/how-to/download-places-and-map-results) and [How to bring in data](/support/how-to/bring-in-data). To put a pin on a place, see [How to pin a place on the map](/support/how-to/pin-a-place-on-the-map).
