# How to Pin a Dashboard and Arrange Your Home Page

## Pin
Open a dashboard and click **Pin to Home**. You can pin up to 12. Only your own pins are shown to you.

## Featured by your organisation
An administrator can feature a shared dashboard for everyone or for chosen people. Featured dashboards show after your own pins.

## Arrange Home
1. Click **Arrange Home**.
2. Move panels up or down, choose a width (4, 6, 8 or 12 of 12 columns on a laptop; full width on a phone) or hide one.
3. **Save**. **Reset** returns to the default.

You can only arrange panels your role is allowed to see.
