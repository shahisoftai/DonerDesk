# How to Pin a Dashboard and Arrange Your Home Page

## Pin
The **Pin to Home** button is offered on older-style dashboards only; the current designs (everything opened from **Dashboards and infographics**) do not have it yet. To get back to a design quickly, open it and click **☆ Bookmark** in the top bar: bookmarks appear on Home in a **Bookmarks** panel (see [How to personalise your workspace](/support/how-to/personalise-your-workspace)). If you do have pinned dashboards, you can pin up to 12, only your own pins are shown to you, and they appear near the top of Home.

## Featured by your organisation
An administrator can feature a shared dashboard for everyone or for chosen people. Featured dashboards show after your own pins.

## Arrange Home
1. Click **Arrange Home**.
2. Move panels up or down, choose a width (4, 6, 8 or 12 of 12 columns on a laptop; full width on a phone) or hide one.
3. **Save**. **Reset** returns to the default.

You can only arrange panels your role is allowed to see.
