# How to Filter, Drill, Present and Print a Dashboard

## Filter
Click **Filters** at the top of the design. The panel offers **Up to period** (**Latest** or a period), **Place**, **Level** (goal, outcome, output) and **Indicator**. Nothing is saved: close it or clear the choice to go back to the design as saved.
- Click a chart label, or use the filter pane, to narrow by **period**, **place**, **group** (a recorded breakdown category) or **status** (planned-work status).
- Use **Level** and **Indicator** to drill into a result. Step back out one level at a time.
- A group shows what was recorded for it, without the project target.
- Nothing is saved. Clear the filter to go back to the design as saved.

## Present and show on a screen
- **Present** opens the design full screen.
- **Tools ▾ → TV display** plays your designs one after another.

## Print and download
Open **More** at the top for everything below:
- **Download as a picture**, **Print this page** (draws every chart first, so nothing is blank), **Make a PDF file**, **Make a PNG file**, **Make a PowerPoint file**, **Make a pack (all files and the text)** and **Download the canvas file**.
- **Download data (.xlsx)** and **Download data (.csv)** give tables of what you see. A gap is empty, and text is never a formula.
- **Email me every week** / **Email me every month** (a title and a link, never figures), **Download descriptions and captions**, **Make a copy for me** and **Delete**.

## Slow connections
When your browser asks for reduced data, charts show as tables and maps load no tiles. Older-style dashboards also have a **Low-data mode** button; the current designs follow your browser's setting.

## Live data
The page checks for new data every 30 seconds while it is visible and refreshes what changed.
