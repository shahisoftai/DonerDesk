# How to Change a Team Member's Role

Only **Admins** can change roles.

## Steps

1. Open **Team** in the left menu. Search by name or email, or filter by role and status.
2. In the person's row, click **Change role**, then pick the **New role**.
3. DonorDesk shows what they **Will gain** and **Will lose**. Review it.
4. Click **Confirm change**.

The change applies the next time the person loads a page. It is recorded in the audit log.

## Project-level assignments

A role is workspace-wide. To control *which projects* someone works on, use the project's **Settings → Team**: **Assign member**, set the **Project role**, or **Remove member**.

## Tips

- Give people the least role they need. Field staff usually need **Field Officer** only.
- Use **Viewer** for board members, donors and auditors: it is read-only and does not use a paid seat.
- Keep at least two Admins so someone can always manage billing and the team. DonorDesk will not let you deactivate or delete the last active Admin.
- To stop someone signing in, use **Deactivate** (reversible) or **Delete** (final) on the same row; see [How to invite team members](/support/how-to/invite-team-members#deactivate-or-delete-someone).

See the full matrix in [User roles and permissions](/support/getting-started/user-roles-and-permissions).
