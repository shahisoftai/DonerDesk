# How to Share, Embed and Email a Dashboard or Infographic

## Link
Open the design and expand **Share and publish** (below the canvas). Under **Share links**, choose what it **Shows** (*The canvas as it stands when opened*, which is the live link, or *One kept copy*, which is the frozen one), set **Lasts (days)** (1 to 180) and click **Make a link**. The address is shown at once, with an embed code; copy it then. A link can be withdrawn with **Withdraw**, after which it answers *This link is not available*.
- **Live** link: draws the design as it stands when opened, as an outside guest.
- **Frozen** link: serves one kept copy.

## What a live link shows
Indicator figures, the plan and results by place can be shown. **Money, budgets and project lists never go through a link.** Movements between places do not either. A link only reaches projects the design names.

## Embed
Copy the `<iframe …>` embed code shown under the link to put the design in another page, without menus. The page it goes on must allow frames.

## Keep a copy
Under **Published copies**, **Keep a PDF copy**, **Keep a PNG copy**, **Keep a PowerPoint copy** or **Keep a SVG copy** makes an immutable file, listed with its date and size and a **Download** link. A shared design must pass the Design check; a donor report design must be approved first.

## Scheduled copies
Under **Scheduled copies**, choose **Every week** or **Every month** to have a PDF copy kept automatically. Each is made as the person who switched it on, and skipped with a reason if the design may no longer be published.

## Email me this
Choose **Email me this**, weekly or monthly. The email has the title and a link, never figures, and you only get it while you can still see the design. **Stop emailing me** ends it.
