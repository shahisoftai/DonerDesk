# How to Share, Embed and Email a Dashboard or Infographic

## Link
In **Publish**, make a link. It expires (you choose how long), can be withdrawn, and shows the token once, so copy it then.
- **Live** link: draws the design as it stands when opened, as an outside guest.
- **Frozen** link: serves one kept copy.

## What a live link shows
Indicator figures, the plan and results by place can be shown. **Money, budgets and project lists never go through a link.** Movements between places do not either. A link only reaches projects the design names.

## Embed
Use the embed address of the link to put the design in another page, without menus.

## Keep a copy
**Publish** makes an immutable file (PDF, PNG, SVG or PPTX). A shared design must pass the Design check; a donor report design must be approved first.

## Scheduled copies
Choose weekly or monthly to have copies kept automatically. Each is made as the person who switched it on, and skipped with a reason if the design may no longer be published.

## Email me this
Choose **Email me this**, weekly or monthly. The email has the title and a link, never figures, and you only get it while you can still see the design. **Stop emailing me** ends it.
