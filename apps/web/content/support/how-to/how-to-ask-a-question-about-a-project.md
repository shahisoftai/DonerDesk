# Ask a question about a project

Ask DonorDesk answers a question from your project's own records and shows where each sentence comes from.

## Before you start

- Your organisation's administrator must turn Ask DonorDesk on: **Settings → Ask DonorDesk → Allow Ask DonorDesk in this organisation**. It is off until then.
- You need to be able to see the project.

## Ask

1. Open the project and choose **Ask**.
2. Type your question in **Your question** (up to 500 characters), or choose one of the examples.
3. Choose **Ask**.

## Read the answer

- Each sentence is followed by the records it rests on: an indicator, an activity, an evidence file, or the project itself. A label such as "not yet verified" means the value is still a draft.
- **What the records do not tell** lists what is missing. If nothing in the records answers the question, you see "The project's records do not answer this question."
- The line at the bottom says how much was looked at and how many sentences were left out. A sentence is left out when it names no record, or quotes a figure the record does not contain.

## What is read and what is sent

Indicator values, accepted activity records and the text of evidence you are allowed to open. Finance is not read. Names, e-mail addresses and phone numbers are removed before the question and records are sent to the AI service. Nothing is sent until you ask.

## Limits

Your organisation can ask 60 questions an hour and 400 a day. Asking is not recorded with the question's text, only its length.
