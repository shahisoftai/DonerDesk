# How to Animate a Chart or a Figure

Motion is optional and never changes a figure.

## Add motion to a block
1. Open the design and click **Edit**.
2. Select a block and open **Motion**.
3. Choose **None**, **Fade in** or **Rise in**. A single figure also offers **Count up**. Charts offer options that grow or draw their marks.

Only motion that suits the block's picture is offered.

## Play a bar race
A **Bar race** chart steps through your periods one at a time. Press **Play** to start and **Stop** to pause; each frame is named by its period.

## Where it does not play
- Files, printouts and PDFs always show the final picture.
- If your device asks for reduced motion, the final picture is shown.
- Chart refreshes after a data change do not replay the introduction.
