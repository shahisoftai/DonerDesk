# How to Invite Team Members

Only **Admins** can invite people and change roles.

## Invite from the Team page

1. Click **Team** in the left menu (under **ADMIN**).
2. Click **Invite member**.
3. Enter the person's **Email** and choose a **Role** (see [Roles and permissions](/support/getting-started/user-roles-and-permissions)). Under **Add them to projects (optional)** tick the projects they should join straight away; when they accept, they are added to those projects.
4. Click **Send invite**. To bring in someone from another organisation, choose **Outside partner**; see [How to invite an outside partner](/support/how-to/invite-an-outside-partner).

An **Invitation created** panel shows the acceptance link (`/invite/accept?token=…`). **DonorDesk does not send invitation emails at the moment**, so copy the link, add your workspace's address in front of it, and send it to the person yourself (email, chat, etc.).

## One email address, one organisation

Sign-in takes only an email and a password, so an address should belong to **one organisation**. If the same address already has an account in another organisation, signing in opens that account, not the one you invited. Invite the person with a different address, or ask them to use the other organisation's address for that work.

## Who has not joined yet

Below the member table, **Invited, not joined yet (n)** lists everyone you invited who has not accepted. Each row shows the email, the role and how long the link is valid (or **Expired**), with a **Copy link** button, so you can send the link again without inviting twice. Only Admins see this list, because the link lets whoever has it join with that role.

## What the invitee does

1. Opens the link (**Join your workspace**).
2. Enters their name and a password (at least 12 characters with no spaces, using three of: lowercase, uppercase, number, symbol).
3. Ticks **I have read and accept the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy** (recorded in the audit trail) and clicks **Accept invitation**, signing in.

They join with the role you chose. Links expire; if it stops working, invite the person again.

## Seats

Full seats (everyone except Viewers) count against your plan: Starter 1, Team 5, Growth 15. Read-only **Viewers** are counted separately (Starter 2, others unlimited). If you are at the limit you must free a seat or upgrade. See [Plans and limits](/support/account-billing/plans-and-limits).

## Assign people to projects

If you did not tick projects when you invited them, invited members do not appear on a project. Open the project → **Settings → Team** → **Assign member**, choose the member and a **project role**. You can **Remove member** to close the assignment.

## Deactivate or delete someone

On **Team**, each member's row has **Change role**, **Deactivate** and **Delete** (you cannot use them on your own account).

- **Deactivate** stops the person signing in. If they are signed in, their session ends on their next click. Nothing they did changes, and they keep their seat. The row shows **SUSPENDED** and the button becomes **Reactivate**, which lets them sign in again with their existing password and role.
- **Delete** is final. The person leaves every project, their name and email are erased from the team list and the seat is freed, so the address can be invited again. Everything they recorded (activities, values, evidence) stays, shown as **Removed user**, and the audit log records that it happened.

Both ask you to confirm in a box that says exactly what will happen. The organisation always keeps at least one active **Admin**: you cannot deactivate or delete the last one.

A pending invitation you no longer want can be withdrawn: in **Invited, not joined yet**, click **Cancel invite** on its row and confirm. The link stops working at once and the seat it held is free again; you can invite the person again later. (Someone who has already joined is deactivated or deleted instead, as above.)

## You can also invite during setup

The **Get started** checklist (**Settings → Workspace setup**) has an **Invite your team** step that opens the same form.
