# How to Turn On Two-Step Sign-In

> Two-step sign-in asks for a six-digit code from an authenticator app after your password. Someone who learns your password still cannot sign in.

## Before you start

You need an authenticator app on your phone, such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy or 1Password.

## Turn it on

1. Go to **Settings → Security**.
2. Select **Set up two-step sign-in**.
3. In your authenticator app, add an account and either scan the code on screen or type the key shown.
4. Enter the six-digit code the app shows, then select **Turn on**.
5. **Save your eight recovery codes now.** Each works once if you lose your phone. They are shown only once.

## Sign in with it

After your password, DonorDesk asks for the code from your app. If you have lost your phone, enter one of your recovery codes instead.

## Turn it off

1. Go to **Settings → Security** and select **Turn off**.
2. Enter your password and a current code, then confirm.

## Good to know

- If your organisation signs in through single sign-on, your identity provider manages two-step sign-in, and the switch is not shown.
- After several wrong passwords, the account is locked for a short time. A correct sign-in, or a password reset, clears the lock.
- Keep the recovery codes somewhere safe that is not your phone.
