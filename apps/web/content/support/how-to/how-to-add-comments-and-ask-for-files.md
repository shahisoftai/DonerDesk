# How to Comment, and What "Files We Will Need" Means

## Comments

On a planned activity, a completed activity, an indicator, or a problem or risk, choose **Add a comment**. Type your note; mention a team member to tell them. The people who own the thing (the lead, the person who entered it, the indicator's collector) are told too. You are never told about your own comment.

## Files we will need

When you plan an activity, DonorDesk suggests the kinds of file that should prove it from its title: a training suggests an attendance sheet and a photo, a distribution suggests a distribution list. Tick or untick what you want; you can ask for up to five kinds. Open **More details** on the planned-activity form, or **Change** on the activity page.

When completed activities are entered for it, the page says **Still needed:** what has no file yet. Nothing is blocked: when you accept a completed activity that is missing a file, you are asked once, and **Accept anyway** always works.
