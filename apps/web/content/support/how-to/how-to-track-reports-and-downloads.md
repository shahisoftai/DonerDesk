# How to Track Every Report and Find Your Downloads

Two pages in the main menu answer "what is due?" and "where is the file I exported?":

- **Reports** shows every report across **all your projects**, with deadlines and how ready each one is.
- **Download Center** keeps every report and file you have exported.

## The Reports page

Click **Reports** in the main menu (under **REPORT**). The page is headed **Cross-project pipeline**: *Deadlines and readiness for every accessible project*.

### Read the list

Each report shows its project, its type and period (for example *Monthly report · Nov 1, 2026 – Deadline Dec 10, 2026*), how many days are left, a **Readiness** percentage and a status such as **Not started** or **Draft generated**.

Tabs across the top count the reports in each group:

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **All** | Every report you can see |
| **Due** | Reports not yet completed |
| **Overdue** | Reports past their deadline |
| **Completed** | Reports that are finished |
| **Ready to download** | Reports with an export ready |

### Find what you need

- **Sort by**: deadline (soonest or latest first), readiness (lowest or highest first), project name, or reporting period (newest first).
- **Group by**: **Project**, **Due / completed**, **Report type** or **No grouping**.
- **Project** and **Report type** filters narrow the list to one project or one kind (for example only **Final report**).

Click a report to open its workspace. From there, see [How to generate an AI report draft](/support/how-to/generate-ai-report-draft).

**Readiness** is a guide to how complete the report's inputs are. A low number on an overdue report tells you where to start. See [Report readiness](/support/getting-started/report-readiness).

## Start your week from My Work

If you only want the reports that are **yours** to act on, open **My Work** instead. It lists everything assigned to you by due date, including late reports. See [How to ask for work and use My Work](/support/how-to/ask-for-work-and-use-my-work).

## The Download Center

Click **Download Center** in the main menu. It is headed **Every report and file you have exported**.

1. Export a report from its page (see [How to export reports](/support/how-to/export-reports)).
2. Open **Download Center**. The export appears in the list, ready to download again whenever you need it.
3. Until you export something, the page says **Nothing has been exported yet**.

You can also open the same list from inside a report: **⋯ → Export center → Past exports and downloads**.

## Tips

- Check **Overdue** first thing, then sort **Readiness: lowest first** to find the reports that need the most work.
- Download Center keeps the exports you have made. If you need a fresh file after changing the report, export again.
