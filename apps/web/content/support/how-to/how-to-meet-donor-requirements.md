# How to Meet Your Donor's Requirements

Donors often require specific sections, annexes and tables in a report, and sometimes ask for an explanation when a result falls well short of its target. DonorDesk checks for these and tells you what is missing.

## Required annexes

If the donor's template lists a required annex, the report must have a section for it. If it does not, approval is blocked and the report names the annex that is missing. Add a section with that name to the report.

## Requirement packs (for Admins)

An Admin can write down what a donor requires, as a list in **Settings → Donor requirements**:

- One requirement per line, starting with its kind and a colon, for example `SECTION: Executive summary` or `ANNEX: Indicator table`.
- End a line with `?` to make it advisory rather than mandatory.
- Lines beginning with `#` are comments.

Save it as a draft, check it, then select **Activate**. If a line cannot be read, the message names its line number; the other lines are kept.

## Donor tables

If the donor requires a table with particular columns and a section does not have it, the section shows a warning that names the table. It is a warning, not a block: add the table to the section, or note why it is not needed.

## Results far from target

On the closing report, an indicator that finished more than 20% below its target (or above, for indicators where lower is better) adds an item called **Result to explain** to the checklist. Explain the result in **Tell the story**, under *Why were important targets over or under achieved?*. The item closes when the explanation is saved.

Rates and indicators with no figure are not checked this way.
