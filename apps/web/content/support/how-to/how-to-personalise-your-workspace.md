# How to Personalise Your Workspace

A few small settings make DonorDesk faster to use every day. They are all yours: none of them changes what anyone else sees.

## Bookmark a page

Use a bookmark for a page you open often, such as one project's Finance tab or a report.

1. Open the page.
2. Click **☆ Bookmark** in the top bar. It turns into **★ Bookmarked** and a small message confirms.
3. Your bookmarks appear on **Home** in a **Bookmarks** panel. Click one to open it, or **×** to remove it.
4. To remove a bookmark from the page itself, click **★ Bookmarked** again.

Good to know:
- A bookmark remembers the page address, including filters in it (so a filtered Reports list is bookmarked as filtered).
- Bookmarks are yours alone, and you can keep up to 30.
- Opening a bookmark shows the page under your own access.

## Switch to dark mode

Click the moon icon in the top bar (**Switch to dark mode**). Click it again to go back. The choice is kept on this device.

## Change the language

Use the **Language** menu in the top bar: **English**, **Français**, **Español** or **العربية**. Arabic reads right to left. The choice is kept on this device, so it works offline too. See [How to change the interface language](/support/how-to/change-the-interface-language).

## Arrange Home

On **Home**, click **Arrange Home** to move, resize or hide panels, and pin dashboards. See [How to pin dashboards and arrange Home](/support/how-to/pin-dashboards-and-arrange-home).

## Choose how you are emailed

Open **Notifications** (the bell, then the full page) and use **Email me**: **Daily summary**, **Only urgent things** or **Never**. See [How to use notifications](/support/how-to/use-the-notification-system).

## Jump anywhere with search

Press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) or click **Search** to find projects and evidence quickly. See [How to search projects and evidence](/support/how-to/search-projects-and-evidence).

## Collapse the menu

Click **«** (**Collapse navigation sidebar**) at the top left to give pages more room. On a phone the menu opens with **Open navigation menu**.
