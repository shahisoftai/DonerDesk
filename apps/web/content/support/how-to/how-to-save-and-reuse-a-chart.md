# How to Save, Version and Reuse a Chart

A saved chart keeps your choices (data, chart type, look), never the figures. It is drawn again from the recorded values each time it is opened, so it cannot go out of date.

## Save a chart
1. Build the chart in the **Charts** tab of the Visual Studio (see [build and style a chart](/support/how-to/use-the-chart-studio)).
2. Click **Save**, give it a name and choose who sees it: **Mine** (only you) or **Project** (everyone who can see the project).
3. Making or changing a **Project** chart needs permission to edit the project. Anyone else can still save a personal one.

## Restore an earlier version
A saved chart keeps its last ten versions. Open **Versions**, choose one and click **Restore**. The old settings appear on screen as an ordinary edit: they become a version again only when you save. A save that changes nothing adds no version.

## If someone else saved first
If another person saved the chart after you opened it, your save is refused rather than overwriting theirs. Reload, then re-apply your change.

## Use it elsewhere
- **Dashboard or design:** use **Add to a dashboard** or add it from the design's editor.
- **Report:** see [add a chart to a report](/support/how-to/add-a-chart-to-a-report).
- **Download:** save the picture or its data from the chart's menu.

## Good to know
- A chart can only use projects you can see.
- Deleting a saved chart does not remove copies already placed on a dashboard.
