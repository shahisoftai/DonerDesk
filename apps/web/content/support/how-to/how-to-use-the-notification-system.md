# How to Use Notifications

DonorDesk tells you when something needs your attention. Click the **bell** in the top bar for the latest items, or open **Inbox → Updates** for the full list. (The old **Notifications** page is now the **Updates** tab of the Inbox.)

## What you are notified about

| Type | When |
|---|---|
| Assignment | You are assigned something. |
| Evidence review | Evidence is waiting for your review. |
| Deadline | A reporting deadline is approaching. |
| Compliance | A checklist item is assigned to you. |
| Report approved | A report you worked on was approved. |
| Report returned | A reviewer requested changes. |
| Export | An export finished. |
| Daily summary | Each morning (in your organisation's time zone): *13 things need you today (13 late)*, with the list. |
| Figure you watch | *A figure you watch needs attention*: an [alert you set](/support/how-to/set-up-a-data-alert) was crossed. |
| Plan reminder | For example *starts soon with no lead*: a planned activity is about to start with nobody in charge. |
| Invitation / Password | Account events. |

## Using the Updates tab

- Notifications are grouped by date.
- Filter **All**, **Unread** or **Read**.
- **Email me** sits at the top of the tab. Choose **Daily summary** (a short note in the morning, only on days something is due or late), **Only urgent things**, or **Never**. You still see everything in DonorDesk whichever you choose.
- Mark items read individually.
- Click a notification to open the related item.

## Delivery

Notifications always appear **inside DonorDesk**: click the **bell** (it shows how many are unread). The **Email me** setting controls the optional morning email. Do not rely on email alone to be told about a deadline or review; check **Home** and the **Inbox** (**To do** and **Updates**). Invitation emails are not sent by DonorDesk: the Team page gives you a link to share (see [How to invite team members](/support/how-to/invite-team-members)).

See also [the daily summary and reminders](/support/reference-daily-summary-and-reminders).
