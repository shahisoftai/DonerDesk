# How to Record a Number, an Activity, a File or an Expense

**Record** is the quickest way to enter data by hand. It is the blue **+ Record** button in the top bar (and at the top of every project page), so you can use it from anywhere without hunting for the right screen. You can also press **R** (when you are not typing in a box) or search for it with **Ctrl K**. On a phone, field roles also find **Record** in the bottom bar.

Use Record when you have **one thing to enter**. To fill in many indicators for a whole month at once, use the [Report inputs sheet](/support/how-to/update-indicator-values) instead.

## Open Record

1. Click **+ Record** in the top bar. A panel slides in from the right (from the bottom on a phone). **Esc** closes it.
2. At the top, check **Project**. If you are inside a project it is already chosen; otherwise pick one.
3. Under **What are you recording?**, choose one of the four options:

| Option | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **A number** | A figure for an indicator |
| **Something we did** | A session, visit or delivery (a completed activity) |
| **A photo or file** | Proof of what happened |
| **An expense** | Money spent, with a receipt |

You only see the options your role may use. For example, someone who cannot enter indicator values does not see **A number**.

Click **‹ Back** at any time to choose a different option.

## Record a number

1. Choose **A number**.
2. Under **Which number?**, type a code or name in **Find by code or name**, or pick from the list (each entry shows the indicator code and name).
3. Type the figure under **How many?** The unit is shown in brackets, for example **(%)**. Leave it empty if you do not have it.
4. Under **Which day?**, choose **Today**, **Yesterday** or **Pick a day**. DonorDesk finds the reporting period that contains the day and says so in green, for example **Counts toward Monthly · Nov 2026**. You never have to choose the period yourself.
5. Optional: open **Add detail** to split the figure (for example by sex), fill **Where the figure came from**, or add **A note (optional)**.
6. Click **Save**. The panel confirms, for example **Saved: OP1-1a saved in Monthly · Nov 2026.** Click **Record another number** to enter the next one, or **Done** to close.

What can stop you:
- **"That day is not in a reporting period yet"**: no reporting period covers that date. If the day is outside the project's dates, pick another day. If the project's period has not been created, ask someone who manages the project to [create it](/support/how-to/create-a-reporting-period).
- **Save is greyed out**: it turns on once the problem shown above it is solved.
- Values are refused if the period's report is already approved or submitted, or if the entry window has closed (unless you may verify values).

The figure is saved as a draft. It is handed in for review according to your project's review mode. See [How to update indicator values](/support/how-to/update-indicator-values).

## Record something we did

Use this for a completed activity: a training, a distribution, a visit.

1. Choose **Something we did**.
2. Optional shortcut: click **Tell it in your own words (type or speak) and fill the form for me**, type or dictate what happened, and the form fills in only what your words state (date, place, participant numbers). Nothing is guessed, and nothing is sent to an AI service.
3. Fill in **Activity title**, **Activity date** (**Today**, **Yesterday** or **Pick a day**), **Location** and **What happened?**
4. Optional: add **Photos and files**. Choose **Add photos or files** (or drop them), or click **Take a photo** on a phone. Photos are made smaller before they upload to save phone storage and data.
5. Optional: open **Add detail** for **Who came** (total, male, female, children, people with disability), the **planned activity** this delivers, the logframe activity, an end date (**Through**), and more notes.
6. Click **Save**. The panel confirms (**Saved: *your title* was saved**) with **Record another activity** and **Done**. Under the date it says which period the activity counts toward, for example **Counts toward Final · Jul 2026**.

The activity then goes to a reviewer, who sees it as *Submitted* and **1 waiting for review** on the **Completed activities** list. See [How to review and accept activities](/support/how-to/review-and-accept-activities). The full form is also on **Projects → project → Plan → Work plan → Completed activities → New activity**; see [How to log activities](/support/how-to/log-activities).

## Record a photo or file

1. Choose **A photo or file**, then click the **A photo or file** button.
2. You land on the evidence upload form for the project. Follow [How to upload evidence](/support/how-to/upload-evidence).

## Record an expense

1. Choose **An expense**, then click the **An expense** button.
2. You land on the project's **Finance** tab. Fill in the **Add an expense** form. See [How to track money in the Finance tab](/support/how-to/use-the-finance-tab).

## Working without a connection

Numbers and activities you save with no internet are kept on your device and sent when you are back online. See [How to work offline and take photos](/support/how-to/work-offline-and-take-photos) and [How to use the field app offline](/support/how-to/use-the-field-app-offline).

## Tips

- If you are unsure which period a date belongs to, pick the date and read the message under it. DonorDesk tells you.
- Record the same day you collect the data. It is easier to remember the details and the file.
- A missing value is never saved as 0. If you did not measure it, leave it empty.
