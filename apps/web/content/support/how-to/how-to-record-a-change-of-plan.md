# How to Record a Change of Plan

> When something changes, write down what changed, why, and what it does to the results. A change written this way can be explained in the report and checked against it.

## When to record one

Record a change when an activity moves, is dropped or replaced, when a target or method changes, or when a delay affects results. Record it when you decide it, not at the end of the year.

## Record it

1. Open the project and go to **Reports → Changes of plan**.
2. Under **Record a change**, fill in:
   - **Day it changed**: the date the change was made or took effect.
   - **What changed**: one or two sentences, for example "Moved borehole drilling to the dry season".
   - **Why**: the reason, for example "Access roads were flooded".
   - **Effect on results** (optional): for example "Two water points slip to the next quarter".
   - **Indicator it touches** and **Report it belongs to** (optional).
3. Select **Record the change**.

The newest change is at the top of the log. Each entry shows its date, what changed, why, its effect and its links.

## Good to know

- The indicator and report links must belong to this project.
- A correction changes only what you edit. Clearing the effect or a link empties it.
- A dated change can be cited when a report statement about the change is checked.
- Changes are kept in the audit trail, including the text before an edit.
