# How to Record Movements Between Places for a Flow Map

A flow map draws movements you record. They are never worked out for you.

1. Open the **Maps** tab and choose the project.
2. Scroll to **Movements between places** and add one: **From** place, **To** place (two different places), what moves in a few words (for example *People, tents…*), **How much** (above zero) and its **Unit**, then **Add movement**. You see *Movement recorded. Draw it with the Flow map chart.*
3. Remove one with **Remove** beside it.

## Draw it
In the **Map charts and saved views** area below the map, choose **Flows between places** under **Data shown**; the **Flow map** is drawn (arrow width is the quantity moved, a dot's size is the total through that place). You can then download it as an image or data.
- Only movements with a pin at both ends are drawn. Others are listed.
- Two different units are refused.
- Movements never appear through a share link.

## Two-indicator maps
A **bivariate map** needs exactly two indicators and places with pins. Without pins it says so.
