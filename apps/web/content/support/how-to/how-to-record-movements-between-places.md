# How to Record Movements Between Places for a Flow Map

A flow map draws movements you record. They are never worked out for you.

1. Open the **Maps** tab and choose the project.
2. Open **Movements** and add one: **from** place, **to** place (two different places), **what moves** in a few words, and a **quantity** above zero with its unit.
3. Remove one with its delete button.

## Draw it
In the Chart Studio choose the **Flows between places** data and a **Flow map**.
- Only movements with a pin at both ends are drawn. Others are listed.
- Two different units are refused.
- Movements never appear through a share link.

## Two-indicator maps
A **bivariate map** needs exactly two indicators and places with pins. Without pins it says so.
