# How to Reopen an Approved Report

An approved report is locked: its text and charts cannot change. If you need to change it after approval, for example to add a chart the donor asked for, an approver can **reopen** it. You do not have to regenerate the whole report.

Who can: **Admin** and **Project Manager** (the people who can approve reports).

## Reopen

1. Open the report (**Projects → project → Reporting → the period**).
2. Click the **⋯** menu and choose **Reopen for changes**. (When you are adding a chart from the Data Center, the same button appears next to the message that the report is locked.)
3. Write **why** the report is being reopened and click **Reopen report**. The reason is required.

This works for approved, exported and submitted reports. A submitted report asks you to confirm that what was sent to the donor does not change. A report that was replaced by a newer version cannot be reopened; reopen the current version instead.

## What happens

- The report goes **back to draft** and its approval is cleared. The period shows as a draft again.
- A banner on the report says it was reopened, when and why.
- **Sections stay approved** until you change them. Editing a section, or adding or changing a chart in it, reopens that section only.
- Exports already made are not changed. The earlier text of any section can be restored from its **History**.
- Reopening is recorded in the audit trail: who, when, why, and who had approved it.

## Approve it again

1. Make your changes.
2. Approve the sections you changed.
3. Send the report for review and approve it. The **second-approver rule** applies again, as for a first approval.

Run the export check again before exporting: the reopened report is a new version of the story.
