# Keep a map for offline use

When you work without a signal, the field app can keep a street map of your project's country on your phone.

1. While you have a connection, open the **field app** (`/field`).
2. Choose your project under **Download for offline use**.
3. Under **Map for offline use**, choose **See the map's size**. The app tells you how big the map is before anything large is downloaded.
4. Choose **Keep the map on this phone**.

The map then works with no network: the streets and place names come from your phone.

## Good to know

- The map holds streets and place names only. No project data, figures or names of people are in it.
- A whole country is kept at lower detail than a district, so the download stays phone-sized.
- The map is removed from the phone when you sign out, together with your offline forms.
- A very large country cannot be kept whole. Zoom in to the part you work in.
