# How to Check an Infographic Before You Publish It

The **Design check** (the line *Design check: …* below the canvas) reads your design against the rules that donors and reviewers look for, and lists what needs fixing in plain words. It says *nothing to fix* when everything passes, or for example *2 things to look at · not ready to share*. Click it to open the list.

## Read the list

Each finding names the block it is about. Click a finding to go to that block.

- **Must fix** stops the design from being published until it is cleared (for example *Describe this picture in a sentence for people who cannot see it*). Examples: a map with no legend, a chart whose axis does not start at zero, a photo without a consent note, a donor submission with a block that has no source.
- **Should fix** and **Tip** do not stop publishing, but a reviewer may ask about them. **Not set up** means a block has nothing to show yet (for example a map with no places). Examples: a title that is only "Infographic", more than three themes, a chart with too many lines to read.

If the line says *nothing to fix*, the design is ready to publish.

## Sources and figures

A donor submission needs a source line on every block that shows figures. Write where the information comes from (for example "Partner reports, September 2026"). If a figure does not match the latest approved report, the check says so.
