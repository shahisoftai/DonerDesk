# How to Use Home and the Inbox

## Home (the portfolio view)

**Home** is the first entry in the left menu and your overview of every project you can see. What you see depends on your role. Two tabs sit across the top: **Home** and **Portfolio** (the full portfolio page; see [How to check your portfolio](/support/how-to/check-your-portfolio)). At the top it greets you and says how many items **need you**, starting with the most urgent (for example "13 reports are overdue"). A line below states the rule: **missing data is shown as unavailable, never as zero.**

Three buttons sit at the top: **Open portfolio**, **Open Inbox** and **Arrange Home** (move or hide panels; see [How to pin dashboards and arrange Home](/support/how-to/pin-dashboards-and-arrange-home)).

The panels, from the top:

- **Needs you** – tiles such as **Reports are overdue**, **Serious problems and risks** and **Compliance blockers**. Each has a link (for example **Open the reports list**, **Open checklist items**) that goes straight to the work.
- **Delivery** – how many projects are on track, need attention or are behind.
- **Reports due in 30 days**, **Open problems and risks**, **Evidence to verify** and **Late planned activities** – one count each, with a second line for the worrying part (how many are overdue, serious, awaiting review, or have indicator values overdue).
- **Money across your projects** – **Obligated**, **Received** and **Spent**, always shown **per currency**. Amounts in different currencies are never added. If you ask for the total in your reporting currency and an exchange rate is missing, it says **Rate needed** and which rate to add under **Settings → Finance**.
- **Where we work** – a map of the places your projects work in, coloured by project health. If your device cannot draw maps, the same information appears as a table. See [How to see your work on a map](/support/how-to/see-your-work-on-a-map).
- **Projects by …** – switch between **Sector**, **Status**, **Country** and **Funder**. Click a group to filter.
- **Project health** – one row per project: **Delivery**, **Results** (% of target), **Budget** (spent against time elapsed), **Risks** and **Next report**.

Your pinned dashboards and bookmarks appear near the top. If you have no projects yet you see **Create your first project**.

## The Inbox

**Inbox** (second in the left menu, or press **G** then **I**) is your personal queue across all projects. It replaces the old My Work and Notifications pages. Three tabs sit under the title:

- **To do** – everything that is yours, by when it is due. The headline counts and the project buttons (**All projects**, then one per project) filter the list. Late items come first under **Late (n)**; each row says what is due and links straight to the thing to do. See [How to ask for work and use the Inbox](/support/how-to/ask-for-work-and-use-my-work).
- **Team** – for managers: how much open and late work each person has. It appears only if your role may see it.
- **Updates** – your notifications (what changed, mentions, reminders). See [How to use notifications](/support/how-to/use-the-notification-system).

The **Waiting to send** button at the top right opens the list of things you saved while offline that have not been sent yet.

## Cross-project pages

The left menu has these entries (what you see depends on your role):

- **Home**, **Inbox**, **Projects** – your overview, your queue, your portfolio with search and filters ([guide](/support/how-to/search-projects-and-evidence)).
- **Dashboards and infographics** – your designs ([guide](/support/how-to/make-a-dashboard)).
- **Reports** – reports across all projects. Its tabs are **Reports**, **Evidence**, **Compliance** and **Download Center** (everything you have exported); see [How to track reports and downloads](/support/how-to/track-reports-and-downloads).
- **Programmes and grants** – funding, for Admins and Grants Officers.
- **Team** and **Settings** – for roles that can manage them.

The **«** button at the top left collapses the left menu for more room; on a phone **☰** opens it. The moon button switches between light and dark. Press **Ctrl K** (Mac **⌘K**) to search or jump anywhere ([how](/support/how-to/search-projects-and-evidence)).

## Tips

- Start each day at the Inbox.
- Use **Project health** to decide which project to work on first.
