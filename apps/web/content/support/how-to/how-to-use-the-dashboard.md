# How to Use the Dashboard and My Work

## Home (the portfolio view)

**Home** is your overview of every project you can see. What you see depends on your role. At the top it greets you and says how many items **need you**, starting with the most urgent (for example "13 reports are overdue"). A line below states the rule: **missing data is shown as unavailable, never as zero.**

Three buttons sit at the top: **Open portfolio** (the full portfolio page), **Open My Work** and **Arrange Home** (move or hide panels; see [How to pin dashboards and arrange Home](/support/how-to/pin-dashboards-and-arrange-home)).

The panels, from the top:

- **Needs you** – tiles such as **Reports are overdue**, **Serious problems and risks** and **Compliance blockers**. Each has a link (for example **Open the reports list**, **Open checklist items**) that goes straight to the work.
- **Delivery** – how many projects are on track, need attention or are behind.
- **Reports due in 30 days**, **Open problems and risks**, **Evidence to verify** and **Late planned activities** – one count each, with a second line for the worrying part (how many are overdue, serious, awaiting review, or have indicator values overdue).
- **Money across your projects** – **Obligated**, **Received** and **Spent**, always shown **per currency**. Amounts in different currencies are never added. If you ask for the total in your reporting currency and an exchange rate is missing, it says **Rate needed** and which rate to add under **Settings → Finance**.
- **Where we work** – a map of the places your projects work in, coloured by project health. If your device cannot draw maps, the same information appears as a table. See [How to see your work on a map](/support/how-to/see-your-work-on-a-map).
- **Projects by …** – switch between **Sector**, **Status**, **Country** and **Funder**. Click a group to filter.
- **Project health** – one row per project: **Delivery**, **Results** (% of target), **Budget** (spent against time elapsed), **Risks** and **Next report**.

Your pinned dashboards and bookmarks appear near the top. If you have no projects yet you see **Create your first project**.

## My Work

**My Work** in the main menu is your personal queue across all projects. It opens on **Mine**; switch to **Team** to see everyone's. The headline counts show **Everything** and **To review**, and the project buttons filter to one project. Late items are grouped first under **Late (n)**; each row says what is due and links straight to the thing to do. See [How to ask for work and use My Work](/support/how-to/ask-for-work-and-use-my-work).

## Cross-project pages

The main menu also has:

- **Projects** – your portfolio with search and filters ([guide](/support/how-to/search-projects-and-evidence)).
- **Reports** – reports across all projects ([guide](/support/how-to/track-reports-and-downloads)).
- **Download Center** – everything you have exported.
- **Evidence** – evidence across all projects.
- **Compliance** – checklist items across all projects.
- **Team** and **Settings** – for roles that can manage them.

The sidebar can be collapsed for more room. Light and dark themes are available with the theme toggle.

## Tips

- Start each day at My Work.
- Use **Project health** to decide which project to work on first.
