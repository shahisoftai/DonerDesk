# Troubleshooting Browser and Performance Issues

## Pages are slow or do not load

1. Reload the page and check your connection.
2. Use an up-to-date Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
3. Disable browser extensions that alter pages (ad blockers, script blockers) for donordesk.online.
4. Clear the site's cookies and cache, then sign in again.

## The report editor feels slow

- Long reports with many sections load more content. Use the **outline** to jump between sections instead of scrolling.
- Close other heavy tabs.
- Keyboard shortcuts (**j / k**, **n**) move faster than the mouse ([list](/support/reference-keyboard-shortcuts)).

## Layout problems

- The sidebar can be collapsed for more room.
- On small screens the outline and inspector collapse; use a larger window for review work.
- Use the theme toggle if colours are hard to read (light and dark themes).

## Uploads stall

Large files take time. Upload fewer at once and keep the tab open. Files up to 100 MB are accepted. See [Troubleshooting evidence upload](/support/troubleshooting/evidence-upload).

## Things do not update

Reload. The report editor auto-saves, but pages such as Home and My Work show data as of the last load.

## Still slow

Email support@donordesk.online with your browser and version, the page and time, and a screenshot.
