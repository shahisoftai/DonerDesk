# Understanding Logframes

A **logframe** (logical framework) is the results framework of your project: what you aim to change, how you will do it, and how you will know. DonorDesk uses it to organise indicators, evidence and report sections.

## The four levels

| Level | Question | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Goal** | What long-term change do we contribute to? | Reduced child malnutrition in District X. |
| **Outcome** | What change do we expect among beneficiaries? | Improved feeding practices. |
| **Output** | What do we deliver? | 400 caregivers trained. |
| **Activity** | What do we do? | Counselling sessions. |

Each item has a **code** (OUT-1), a title and a parent. Items form a tree you can rearrange by drag-and-drop.

## Indicators

Each item can have indicators: number, percentage, yes/no, text, ratio, currency or custom. Each has baseline, target, unit and frequency, and optionally data source and means of verification, disaggregation and a calculation rule. See [Indicators and targets](/support/getting-started/indicators-and-targets).

## Why it matters

- The project is not reporting-ready without indicators.
- Evidence and activities link to indicators, which is how reports show proof.
- Verified indicator values feed the report's tables, charts and comparisons.

## Getting one in

Import from Excel/CSV (download the template on the Logframe page) or build it by hand. See [How to build a logframe](/support/how-to/build-logframe).
