# User Roles and Permissions

Every person in a workspace has one **role**. The role decides what they can see and do. DonorDesk has seven roles.

| Role | Typical person | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| **Admin** | Head of programmes, operations lead | Everything, including team, billing, settings and audit log. |
| **Project Manager** | Programme manager | Runs projects: logframe, templates, evidence, reports, approval and export. |
| **M&E Officer** | Monitoring and evaluation | Logframe, indicators (enter and verify), evidence, activities, drafting and editing reports. |
| **Grants Officer** | Donor reporting / grants | Templates, evidence upload, generating, editing and exporting reports, confidentiality overrides. |
| **Field Officer** | Field staff | Fill in the forms given to them, log activities, take photos and upload evidence. Sees only their own work (see below). |
| **Compliance Officer** | Compliance / risk | Verify evidence, manage the compliance checklist, view the audit log. |
| **Viewer** | Board, donor liaison, auditor | Read-only: browse projects and export reports. |

## What a Field Officer sees

A Field Officer is shown only what field work needs, on every screen and in the data the app serves:

- **Menu:** Home, Inbox and Projects.
- **Home:** **Given to you** (the facilities or places assigned to them, one tap to open the form) and **My week**.
- **A project:** opens on its **Forms**; the tabs are **Plan** (the work plan, list and map of their activities) and **Data** (the forms). Photos go in through **Record → A photo or file**.
- **A form:** the **Fill in** tab, with only the facilities given to them.
- **People reached:** they see and change only the people they registered themselves.
- **Files:** they open ordinary files and the ones they uploaded, never other people's **Sensitive** or **Highly sensitive** files.
- **Their phone:** the offline download holds only their own places (their facilities, the project's plan places and the areas above them), not the organisation's whole list.
- **The server agrees with the screens:** anything outside their work is refused ("This is not part of your work."), even if someone tries the address directly.
- Everything else (finance, reports, settings, team, results analysis, the evidence library, dashboards) shows **Page not found**.

Two more things sit outside the table. **Record** shows only the kinds of entry a person's role allows (a number needs the right to enter indicator values; an activity needs the right to log activities). And an **outside partner** is not a role: it is a Field Officer or Viewer who sees only the projects you choose and only a short list of screens (see [How to invite an outside partner](/support/how-to/invite-an-outside-partner)).

Plans limit seats: **Viewers** are counted separately and cheaply from full seats (see [Plans and limits](/support/account-billing/plans-and-limits)).

## Project assignments

On each project's **Settings → Team** you assign workspace members to that project with a **project role**. Access to a project's data follows these assignments as well as the workspace role.

See [How to invite team members](/support/how-to/invite-team-members) and [How to change a member's role](/support/how-to/manage-team-roles-permissions).
