# What is DonorDesk?

DonorDesk is a project and programme management suite for NGOs, UN implementing partners and other grant-funded organisations. You run the programme in DonorDesk: the logframe, work plan, field data collection, indicators, activities, risks, evidence, maps, dashboards, compliance and team. Donor reports are then produced from that record, checked against it, and approved by a person before they go to a donor.

## Why it exists

Most programme information lives in several places at once: indicators in one spreadsheet, activities in another, evidence in folders and chat threads, compliance in an email from the donor. Each reporting deadline, someone rebuilds the same numbers from those sources. The report takes weeks, the figures are hard to trace back to their evidence, and compliance gaps are found on the day of submission.

DonorDesk keeps the record once, while the work happens. The report is then a review of that record, not a rebuild.

## What you do in DonorDesk

DonorDesk covers the whole life of a project or programme. Every part reads from the same record.

**Set up**
1. **Set up the project.** Name the programme, the donor, the reporting periods and the team. Group projects under [programmes and grants](/support/how-to/set-up-programmes-and-grants). Copy a logframe from an earlier project if the design is the same.
2. **Build the logframe and indicators.** Goals, outcomes, outputs and activities, with baselines, targets (including [a schedule by period](/support/how-to/set-a-target-schedule)) and how each indicator is measured. Every change to the framework is kept as a [version](/support/how-to/see-framework-history).

**Plan and deliver**
3. **Plan the work.** [Plan activities](/support/how-to/plan-your-activities) with dates and a lead, move them on the [Timeline](/support/how-to/use-the-timeline), and see what is late. Planning is optional.
4. **Ask for work and follow it.** [My Work and requests](/support/how-to/ask-for-work-and-use-my-work) gather what each person has to do; [My week](/support/how-to/use-my-week) is the field officer's view. [Record problems and risks](/support/how-to/record-problems-and-risks).
5. **Record activities.** Field staff log what happened: date, place, participants and a narrative, typed or dictated. Managers accept or send back records.
6. **Collect data.** Build a [collection form](/support/how-to/collect-answers-with-a-form), use the [field app without a connection](/support/how-to/use-the-field-app-offline), or [bring in KoboToolbox or SurveyCTO submissions](/support/how-to/bring-in-kobotoolbox-or-surveycto-submissions).
7. **Keep the evidence.** Files can stay in your own Google Drive or in DonorDesk storage. Link each file to the activity or indicator it proves.

**Measure and show**
8. **Enter indicator values** once per reporting period, in the Data Center or on the period's inputs page, then [submit them for review](/support/how-to/update-indicator-values).
9. **See it.** The [Data Center](/support/how-to/use-the-data-center), [dashboards](/support/how-to/make-a-dashboard), [maps](/support/how-to/see-your-work-on-a-map), results by place and the project Overview show where you stand. [Infographics](/support/how-to/make-an-infographic) turn it into a one-page design, and [Ask DonorDesk](/support/how-to/ask-a-question-about-a-project) answers questions from your records when an administrator turns it on.

**Report and prove**
10. **Watch the checklist.** The compliance checklist and readiness score show what the donor requires that is still missing.
11. **Upload the donor template.** DonorDesk extracts its sections, instructions and required tables. You review and approve the structure.
12. **Draft the report.** Each section is drafted from the record and cites its sources. Tables and charts are built from verified data. You edit the drafts like a document.
13. **Approve and export.** A second approver can be required. Export Word, PDF, Excel or an evidence pack (ZIP). You send the approved file to the donor yourself.

**Connect**
14. **Open it up.** [API keys, webhooks and an AI-assistant server](/support/how-to/connect-api-keys-webhooks-and-ai-assistants) are read-only and scoped. Use the navigation, field app and collection forms in [English, French, Spanish or Arabic](/support/how-to/change-the-interface-language).

## Who uses it

- **Project Managers** plan the work, run the programme and review activities.
- **M&E Officers** own the indicators and the Data Center.
- **Grants Officers** track the deadlines and the donor requirements.
- **Field Officers** record activities, fill forms and capture evidence, online or offline.
- **Compliance Officers** check the checklist and the evidence.
- **Admins** set up the workspace, the team and the billing.
- **Viewers** read projects and reports, for board members, donors' reviewers and auditors.
- **Outside partners** are invited to named projects only.

Local and national NGOs, international NGOs, UN implementing partners, consultancies and government programme units use DonorDesk across humanitarian, development, health, nutrition, WASH, protection, education, livelihoods, shelter, food security and climate work.

## Principles

- **People decide.** AI drafts; people verify, edit and approve. Nothing is released without an approval.
- **Evidence first.** Every statement in a report can be traced to an activity, an indicator value or a piece of evidence.
- **Your data stays yours.** Files can stay in your own Google Drive. Each organisation's data is separated from every other's. Every change is recorded in an audit log.

## What DonorDesk does not do

- It does not send reports to donors. You upload the approved file yourself.
- It is not a finance or accounting system. Finance figures appear only when switched on for a project, and a verified summary reaches a report.
- It can collect data with its own forms, and it can bring in submissions from KoboToolbox and SurveyCTO; it does not replace a full survey platform.
- The app needs an internet connection to open, except the field app at /field, which works offline once a project is downloaded.
