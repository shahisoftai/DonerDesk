# Donor Reporting Guidelines Index

> "There is no single universal reporting standard used by every donor. Reporting requirements depend on: (1) the donor, (2) the funding mechanism, (3) the grant or contribution agreement, (4) the reporting period and report type, and (5) the specific template version attached to the award."

This index covers the principal publicly available donor reporting guidance as of August 2026.

## Common Reporting Requirements

Across all major donors, the recurring content requirements are:

- Reporting period, project identity and award number
- Executive summary
- Progress against outcomes, outputs and indicators
- Planned versus achieved results
- Explanation of variances and underperformance
- Activity implementation narrative
- Beneficiary numbers and disaggregation
- Geographic coverage
- Monitoring methodology and data quality
- Evidence or means of verification
- Risks, assumptions, constraints and mitigation measures
- Safeguarding, protection, gender, disability and inclusion
- Accountability to affected populations
- Complaints, fraud, incidents and compliance disclosures
- Partnerships and localisation
- Budget against expenditure and forecast
- Value for money or cost efficiency
- Changes requiring donor approval
- Lessons learned and adaptive management
- Sustainability, exit or transition arrangements
- Communications and visibility compliance
- Annexes: indicator tables, financial statements, case studies, photographs
