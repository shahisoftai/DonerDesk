# Reference: Passwords and Sign-in Lockout

## Password rules

New passwords (when you sign up, accept an invitation, change your password or reset it) must:

- be at least **12 characters** long,
- contain at least **three** of: a lowercase letter, an uppercase letter, a number, a symbol,
- have **no spaces**.

If your password does not meet the rules, the form says which rule it breaks.

## Sign-in lockout

After **five wrong passwords in a row**, sign-in to that account pauses for **15 minutes**. The message says how long to wait. A correct sign-in or a completed password reset clears the count.

An unknown email address gets the same message as a wrong password, so the sign-in page cannot be used to find out which addresses have accounts.

If you are locked out and cannot wait, use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page to set a new password.

## Messages

| Message | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Invalid credentials* | The email or password is wrong, or the address has no account | Check both, or use Forgot your password? on the sign-in page |
| *Too many failed sign-in attempts. Try again in N minutes…* | The account is paused | Wait, or reset your password |
| *Password must be at least 12 characters* (and similar) | The new password breaks a rule | Choose a longer password with the required mix |

Two-factor sign-in is not available yet.
