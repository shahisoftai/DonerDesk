# Team Management

Everything about your people in one place.

## Team page (Admins)

- **Invite member** with an email and role. Share the acceptance link yourself (emails are not sent automatically). See [invite team members](/support/how-to/invite-team-members).
- Search by name or email and filter by role.
- Change a role with **New role**, reviewing what the person will gain or lose.

## Project team (each project)

- **Assign member** with a **project role**.
- **Remove member** to close the assignment.
- Assign only the people who work on the project.

## Seats and plans

Full seats: Starter 1, Team 5, Growth 15. Viewers are separate. Invited and suspended members count as seats. See [Plans and limits](/support/account-billing/plans-and-limits).

## Onboarding and offboarding

- Onboarding: [How to onboard a team member](/support/how-to/onboard-team-member).
- Offboarding: change the person's role to **Viewer** or ask support@donordesk.online to remove them, and end their project assignments. Their past actions remain in the audit log.

## Good practice

- Keep two Admins.
- Give the least role needed.
- Review the team every quarter.
