## Purpose
To reduce acute malnutrition among children under five and PLW. The programme does this through:
- early detection by community MUAC screening
- treatment of severe acute malnutrition (SAM) in OTP and stabilisation centres
- treatment of moderate acute malnutrition (MAM) in TSFP/SFP
- IYCF counselling and micronutrient supplementation (MNP, MMN, IFA)

A scale-up in high-burden districts runs from April 2027.

## Performance standards (Sphere)
The rates are calculated from admissions and exits. They are never summed across districts.
- Cure rate: above 75%
- Death rate: below 10%
- Default rate: below 15%
