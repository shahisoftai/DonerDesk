Three colour brands for DonorDesk 4.000

Each option shows the palette, the app (light and dark) with buttons, links, a section and the new right-side panel, the marketing site's first screen, and the eight chart colours. Every text and button colour was chosen to pass WCAG AA on its background. Pick one, or ask to mix parts.
