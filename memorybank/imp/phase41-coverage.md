## Honest limits

- Admin 4 exists only where an open source publishes it; a country without it stops at the finest level in its row above, and the map says so ("Finest boundaries available for ...").
- Boundaries are simplified for drawing (about 100 m at admin 2 and 3, about 10 m at admin 4). They are for finding where work happens, not for surveying or settling a border.
- An organisation can upload its own boundaries for a country and level (Settings); those are shown only to that organisation.
