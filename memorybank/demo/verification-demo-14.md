## Findings
- F1 A new organisation has "Set up projects from a document" off; the wizard says so clearly, an admin must switch it on in Settings.
- F2 Onboarding sent the browser straight to Google's Drive consent; the user had to complete it.
- F3 Wizard read 26 indicators twice (52 proposed): the .docx table and the .xlsx were not merged (same as demo 13 F8).
- F4 Indicators read from the document arrive with "Kind of number" and "Responsible"/"Better when" unset for 17 of 26 (type word NUMBER not applied); the slide only says "missing its type, frequency, baseline or target".
- F5 Places: bundled areas are the old 14 zones; districts sit under them (Bajura under Karnali zone). The slide stays red until an area is ticked or "not used".
- F6 Work plan: selecting places/indicators on a card in quick succession loses clicks (12 of 26 indicators fed after the first pass); slower real clicks worked.
- F7 Reporting slide: sector starting answers add Quarterly and Annual reports to a 6-month project; Semi-annual and Final are not listed after launch and April's period is not created (demo 13 F22 again).
- F8 "Starting dashboard: Done" at launch but no dashboard exists (demo 13 F25 again); creating from a template shows "Your dashboard is ready" only at the bottom of a very long page and the URL does not change, so repeated clicks make duplicates (up to 3 here).
- F9 Activity form (Phase 44): only 3 planned activities are suggested, so most records stay unlinked and the report story says "12 of 12 planned activities were not held".
- F10 KPI tiles: "Down 1,800 since Mar 2027" for a monthly count and "0 facilities, 100% of target" (cumulative target vs latest period) read as contradictory.
- F11 Theme flipped to dark during the session without a toggle by the driver (check the theme persistence).
