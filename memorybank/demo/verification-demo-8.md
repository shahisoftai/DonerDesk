## The project
**[DEMO-8] Safe Water, Sanitation and Hygiene for Displaced and Host Communities in Borno State (BORNO-WASH)** — donor Lake Chad Basin Resilience Fund (fictional, grant LCBRF/NG/2026/WASH-031),
implementer Borno Community Water Initiative, partner Konduga Women's Development Association; Jere, Konduga, Mafa, Dikwa LGAs; 2026-02-01 → 2026-09-30; USD 720,000; project id `c2741532-4270-496a-82ff-cb9eb92893ac`.
Reports: Feb, Mar monthly; **Apr–Jun quarterly**; Jul, Aug monthly; Sep = **Final** (closing); plus an August **Situation** report (floods and cholera).
Story: Feb start-up (contractor, Ramadan), Mar first boreholes, Apr Mafa road closure, May rig breakdown, Jun first rains collapse pits, Jul cholera alert, Aug floods contaminate water points, Sep handover; water points finish at 16 of 20 (honest shortfall).
14 indicators incl. one lower-is-better (diarrhoea prevalence), 6 rates, 2 with sex breakdown; 17 logframe items; 3 donor templates (monthly DOCX, quarterly PDF, final DOCX); 42 evidence files.
