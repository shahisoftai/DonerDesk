# How to Manage Currency Settings

DonorDesk supports multiple currencies, allowing you to accept donations from international donors in their preferred currency.

## Setting Your Primary Currency

Your primary currency is set during initial setup. To change it:
1. Go to **Settings > Organisation**
2. Find **Primary Currency**
3. Select your currency from the dropdown
4. Save

Your primary currency is used for:
- Financial reports and totals
- Tier amounts (if not using Creem)
- Dashboard metrics

## Accepted Currencies

DonorDesk accepts donations in most major currencies:
- USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD
- JPY, CNY, INR
- BRL, MXN
- And many more

When a donor selects a non-primary currency, the donation is converted at the current exchange rate at the time of processing.

## Display Currency on Forms

Configure how currency appears on your donation forms:
1. Go to **Settings > Donation Forms**
2. Edit your form
3. Find **Currency Display**
4. Choose:
   - **Primary currency only** — all amounts shown in your currency
   - **Donor's local currency** — amounts shown in donor's selected currency
   - **Both** — show both with conversion

## Currency Conversion

When a donation is made in a non-primary currency:
1. The original amount and currency are stored
2. The converted amount in your primary currency is calculated
3. Both values are displayed on the donation record
4. Reports can be filtered by original or converted amounts

## Multi-Currency Reporting

Reports can be viewed in:
- Primary currency (all converted)
- Original currencies (actual amounts)
- Both side-by-side

For tax advice related to multi-currency donations in your jurisdiction, consult a local tax professional. For help with currency settings, contact support@donordesk.online.
