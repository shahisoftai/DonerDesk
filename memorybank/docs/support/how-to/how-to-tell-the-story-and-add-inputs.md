# How to Tell the Story and Add Report Inputs

Numbers do not explain themselves. **Tell the Story** (step 2 of the reporting workspace) is where you add the context that the AI uses to write a convincing report. Open the **Data & story inputs** page from **⋯ → Edit data & story** in the editor or from the period workspace.

Report writers can edit it; others can read it.

## The story questions

- **What challenges did you face?** What slowed or blocked the work.
- **Why were important targets over or under achieved?** The donor will ask about big gaps between target and result.
- **What changed or was adapted?** Changes to plans, locations or methods.
- **Any important lesson or story? (optional)** A short example makes the report memorable.

Changes save automatically. If you leave with unsaved changes you are warned.

## Other inputs on the same page

- **Indicator values** – edit or import them ([guide](/support/how-to/update-indicator-values)).
- **Evidence** – shows what is linked to the period; you are told if none is.
- **Field reports and CSV** – paste a field report (text from a DOCX/PDF) or CSV such as `Indicator code,Period achievement`. DonorDesk **proposes inputs that you confirm** before anything is saved.

## Before you generate

The launch card checks: indicator figures present, story written, evidence linked, and whether figures are verified. Unverified figures are still used but **marked in the report**.

If you change these inputs after a draft exists, the editor shows **Inputs changed**.
