# Use the field app without a connection

The field app lets you fill in a project's forms on a phone with no signal. Your answers are kept on the phone and sent when it reconnects.

## Before you go

1. Open **Work offline** on a project's **Collection forms** page (under **Data**), or go to `/field`, while you are online.
2. Choose a project under **Download for offline** and choose **Download or refresh**.

The download holds the project's form questions and the list of places. It holds no records, figures or people. It is removed when you sign out. A download older than two weeks shows a reminder to refresh it, but still works.

## Fill in a form offline

Open the form from **On this phone**, fill it in and choose **Save on this phone**. Photos are kept with the answers. A banner shows what is waiting. When the phone is online again the answers are sent; a photo goes up first.

If the server refuses an answer (for example the form was closed in the meantime), the answer is kept and marked for attention with the reason. It is not sent again by itself.

## Location

**Use my location** reads the phone's position, which works without a connection. If location is switched off for the page, type the numbers instead.
