# How to Handle Failed Payments

Failed payments can occur for various reasons. DonorDesk provides tools to identify, retry, and resolve payment failures quickly.

## Why Payments Fail

Common reasons include:
- Card expired or cancelled
- Insufficient funds
- Incorrect card details
- Bank declined the transaction
- Expired authorisation hold

## Viewing Failed Payments

1. Go to **Donations**
2. Click the **Failed** filter tab
3. See all failed payments with failure reason

You can also set up a dashboard widget to show failed payments daily.

## Automatic Retry

DonorDesk automatically retries failed payments based on your retry schedule:
- Day 1: First retry
- Day 3: Second retry
- Day 7: Final retry

The donor receives email notifications before each retry attempt.

## Manual Retry

To manually retry a failed payment:
1. Open the donation record
2. Click **Retry Payment**
3. The donor will receive an updated payment email
4. Track the retry status in real-time

## Notifying the Donor

For persistent failures, manually reach out:
1. Open the donor profile
2. Click **Log Interaction**
3. Select "Payment Issue" as the reason
4. Add notes about the failed payment
5. Choose to email the donor directly with an update link

## Updating Payment Method

If the donor needs to update their card:
1. Send them a secure payment update link
2. They can update their card without creating a new donation
3. Once updated, retry the original donation

## Writing Off a Failed Payment

If the payment cannot be recovered:
1. Open the donation record
2. Click **Write Off**
3. Select a reason (fraudulent, abandoned, etc.)
4. The donation is marked as written off
5. It remains in your records for reconciliation

For help with failed payments, contact support@donordesk.online.
