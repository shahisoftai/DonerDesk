# How to Manage Billing and Subscription

This guide covers how to view your current plan, upgrade, downgrade, and manage payment details.

## Accessing Billing Settings

Only the **Owner** of the workspace can manage billing.

1. Click your name → **Settings**
2. Click **Billing**

## What You Will See

The billing page shows:
- Current plan (Starter, Team, Growth, or Enterprise)
- Current billing period (monthly or annual)
- Next billing date
- Amount due
- Storage usage
- AI credits used this month

## Upgrading Your Plan

### Step 1: Go to Billing

1. In **Settings → Billing**, click **Upgrade Plan**

### Step 2: Choose Your New Plan

Select **Team** or **Growth**.

### Step 3: Choose Billing Interval

| Option | Cost | Savings |
|--------|------|--------|
| **Monthly** | Pay each month | No commitment |
| **Annual** | Pay once a year | Saves 2 months |

### Step 4: Checkout

1. You are redirected to a secure checkout page
2. Enter your payment details
3. Click **Pay**

Your upgrade takes effect **immediately**.

You are charged the prorated difference for the remainder of the current month.

## Downgrading Your Plan

### Step 1: Go to Billing

1. In **Settings → Billing**, click **Change Plan**

### Step 2: Select the Lower Plan

Click **Downgrade to Starter** or the plan you want.

### Step 3: Confirm

Downgrading takes effect at the **end of your current billing period**. You keep all features of your current plan until then.

**Note:** If you have more projects or team members than the lower plan allows, you will need to archive or remove the excess before the downgrade takes effect.

## Cancelling Your Subscription

### Step 1: Go to Billing

1. In **Settings → Billing**, click **Cancel Subscription**

### Step 2: Confirm

Your access continues until the end of your current billing period. After that:
- You are moved to the **Starter (free) plan**
- Your data is preserved
- Your projects become read-only (you cannot create new ones until you upgrade again)

### Step 3: What Happens to Your Data

Nothing is deleted. Your evidence, reports, and project data remain accessible on the Starter plan.

## Updating Payment Method

### Step 1: Go to Billing

1. Click **Update Payment Method**

### Step 2: Enter New Details

Update your credit card or payment information.

### Step 3: Save

Your new payment method will be used for future billing.

## Viewing Invoices

1. In **Settings → Billing**, click **Invoice History**
2. See a list of all past payments
3. Click any invoice to view or download it as PDF

Invoices are also sent to your registered email address.

## Annual vs Monthly Billing

| | Monthly | Annual |
|--|--------|--------|
| Cost | $59/month (Team) | $590/year (Team) = $49.17/month |
| Pay | Every month | Once a year |
| Cancel anytime | Yes | Yes (at end of year) |
| Commitment | None | 1 year |

Annual billing is recommended for organisations that know they will use DonorDesk for a full year.

## Nonprofit Discount

If your organisation qualifies for a nonprofit discount:
1. Contact **support@donordesk.online**
2. Provide your nonprofit registration details
3. Once approved, a discount code will be applied to your account

## AI Credit Usage

Each month, your AI credit usage is shown in Billing:

- **Credits used** — How many you have consumed this month
- **Credits remaining** — How many you have left
- **Resets on** — The date your monthly quota resets

Credits reset on the 1st of each month.

If you run out of credits, AI generation will pause until the next month or you upgrade to a plan with more credits.
