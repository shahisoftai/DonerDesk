# How to Create a DonorDesk Account

## What You Need

Before you start, have the following ready:
- A valid work email address (not a personal email like gmail.com if possible)
- Your organisation's name, type, and country
- The sector you work in (e.g., Health, Nutrition, WASH)
- A strong password (at least 8 characters)

## Step-by-Step

### Step 1: Go to the Signup Page

1. Open your browser and go to **https://donordesk.online**
2. Click **Get started** in the top right corner
3. Click **Sign up here** on the login page

### Step 2: Fill in Your Details

Complete the signup form:

**Your information:**
- **Full name** — Enter your first and last name
- **Work email** — Use your organisation email address
- **Password** — Choose a strong password you will remember

**Organisation information:**
- **Organisation name** — Your NGO's or programme's official name
- **Organisation type** — Select from:
  - Local NGO
  - National NGO
  - INGO (International NGO)
  - UN implementing partner
  - Consulting firm
  - Government programme unit
  - Other
- **Country** — Select the country where your organisation is based
- **Primary sector** — Select your main sector:
  - Nutrition
  - Food Security
  - Health
  - WASH
  - Education
  - Protection
  - Livelihoods
  - Shelter
  - Multi-sector
  - Other

### Step 3: Agree to Terms

- Read the **Terms of Service**
- Read the **Privacy Policy**
- Tick the **I agree** checkbox

### Step 4: Create Your Account

Click **Create account**.

### Step 5: Verify Your Email

1. Check your email inbox (and spam folder)
2. You will see an email from **DonorDesk** with the subject "Verify your email"
3. Click the **Verify email** button in the email
4. You will be redirected to DonorDesk and logged in

**If you do not see the email within 5 minutes:**
- Check your spam/junk folder
- Make sure you entered the correct email address
- Try the **Resend verification email** option on the login page

## Step 6: Complete the Setup Wizard

After logging in for the first time, you will see the setup wizard. This guides you through:

1. **Connect Google Drive** (optional) — Links your organisation's Google Drive for file storage
2. **Organisation Profile** — Confirms your organisation details
3. **Default Reporting Profile** — Sets preferences for how reports are structured
4. **Invite Your Team** — Adds your colleagues (you can skip this and do it later)
5. **Accept Terms** — Final confirmation of the Terms of Service

Work through the steps you need. You can skip optional steps and return later.

## After Signup: What Happens Next

Once your account is created:

- Your **organisation workspace** is created
- You are the **Owner** of this workspace (full admin access)
- You start on the **Starter (free) plan**
- You can create up to 1 project straight away

## Troubleshooting Signup Issues

### "This email is already registered"

You may already have an account. Try:
- Logging in instead of signing up
- Using the **Forgot password** link if you do not remember your password

### "Password must be at least 8 characters"

Your password needs to be at least 8 characters long. Choose a longer password with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols.

### "Organisation name is required"

The organisation name field cannot be left blank. Fill in your organisation's full name.

### Verification email not received

1. Wait 5-10 minutes — it may take time to arrive
2. Check your spam/junk folder
3. Make sure your email inbox is not full
4. Contact **support@donordesk.online** if you still do not receive it

## Signing Up with Google

If your organisation uses Google Workspace:

1. Click **Sign up with Google** on the signup page
2. Select your Google account
3. Grant DonorDesk permission to access your basic Google info
4. You will be redirected back to DonorDesk with your account created

Note: Google Sign-Up currently works for existing accounts only. New sign-ups via Google are being developed.
