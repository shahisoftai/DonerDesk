# How to Check an Infographic Before You Publish It

The **Check** tab reads your design against the rules that donors and reviewers look for, and lists what needs fixing in plain words.

## Read the list

Each finding names the block it is about. Click a finding to go to that block.

- **Something to fix** stops the design from being published until it is cleared. Examples: a map with no legend, a chart whose axis does not start at zero, a photo without a consent note, a donor submission with a block that has no source.
- **Worth a look** does not stop publishing, but a reviewer may ask about it. Examples: a title that is only "Infographic", more than three themes, a chart with too many lines to read.

If the list says **Every check passes**, the design is ready to publish.

## Waive a finding

Sometimes a finding is correct for your situation. Click **Publish anyway, with a reason** under the finding, type why it is acceptable (for example "the map is for internal use only"), then click **Waive for this design**. The button stays disabled until you type a reason. The reason stays with the design and is recorded in the audit trail.

## Sources and figures

A donor submission needs a source line on every block that shows figures. Write where the information comes from (for example "Partner reports, September 2026"). If a figure does not match the latest approved report, the check says so.
