# How to Process Refunds

DonorDesk provides a streamlined refund process that maintains full audit trail compliance and notifies all relevant parties automatically.

## When to Issue a Refund

Common reasons for issuing refunds include:
- Donor accidentally submitted a duplicate donation
- Wrong amount was donated
- Donor requested cancellation within your policy window
- Payment was fraudulent (disputed transaction)

## How to Issue a Refund

1. Navigate to **Donations** in the left sidebar
2. Find the original donation using search or filters
3. Click on the donation to open its detail page
4. Click the **Issue Refund** button
5. Select the refund reason from the dropdown
6. Confirm the refund amount (full or partial)
7. Click **Confirm Refund**

The refund is processed through the original payment method. Refunds typically appear within 5-10 business days depending on the payment provider.

## Partial Refunds

For partial refunds:
1. Open the donation detail page
2. Click **Issue Refund**
3. Select **Partial Refund**
4. Enter the specific amount to refund
5. Add a note explaining the reason
6. Confirm the partial refund

## Refund Notifications

When a refund is processed:
- The donor receives an automatic email confirmation
- The donation is marked as "Refunded" in your records
- Your audit log records the full transaction detail
- Your donation reports update automatically

## Refund Policies

Work with your team to establish clear refund policies that comply with applicable regulations. Document your policy and ensure all team members follow the same procedures. Contact support@donordesk.online for help establishing refund workflows.
