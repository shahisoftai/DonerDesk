# Make and share a dashboard

A dashboard is a page of charts from the Data Center. It keeps which charts you chose and where they sit. It does not keep the figures: each chart is drawn from the project's recorded values when you open the dashboard.

## Make one

1. Click **Dashboards and infographics** in the left menu, then **New design** (or **Start from a template**).
2. Under **What do you want to do?** click the goal closest to yours: **Show how a project is progressing**, **Give a donor a one-page summary**, **Send a quick update**, **Show who we have reached**, **Show funding and spending**, **Show who does what, where**, **Follow field operations** or **Score a sector's results**. (Or skip the goal and choose a starter or **A blank page** directly.)
3. Choose **Start from**: a starter (general ones, and one set for each sector) or **A blank page** (*add blocks one at a time*). Starters name roles such as "headline indicator", never figures.
4. Fill in the form under the starters:
   - **Name**
   - **Who is it for**: **Only me**, **Everyone on one project** or **The whole organisation**. Shared designs need someone who manages projects.
   - **Project**: choose the project it reads. (A project with no indicators says so: blocks that need an indicator will be left out.)
   - **First indicator** to **Fourth indicator** (the second to fourth are optional): choose which of the project's indicators fill the starter's roles, or leave them on **None**.
5. Click **Make it**. The design opens ready to edit.

You can also start from your sector's template under **Templates** (see [How to start a dashboard from a template](/support/how-to/start-a-dashboard-from-a-template)). To edit blocks, see [How to work with the canvas editor](/support/how-to/work-with-the-canvas-editor).

## Add a chart

1. Open a project's **Data** tab and build a chart in the Chart Studio (the **Charts** tab). See [Build and style a chart in the Chart Studio](/support/how-to/use-the-chart-studio) for the 50 chart types, the 40 themes and the style options.
2. Under **Use in a dashboard**, choose a dashboard and add the chart. It is added at the bottom of the dashboard, with its theme and style. (Before 9 November 2026 a chart added from the Studio to a dashboard that had already been opened in the newer **Dashboards and infographics** view could be missing from it; it now appears.)

A zoom bar or save-as-picture button you turn on in the Studio shows on the dashboard, but never in a printed or downloaded copy.

## Arrange

Choose **Edit**. Drag a chart by its title bar, or resize it from its bottom-right corner. You can also focus the title bar and use the arrow keys to move a chart, and Shift with an arrow key to resize it. Charts move out of the way for each other and never overlap. Choose **Save** to keep the layout, or **Cancel** to drop it.

If someone else saved the dashboard while you were editing, your save is refused and nothing is merged. Reload the page and make your changes again.

## Share a read-only link

1. Open the dashboard and choose **Make a link** under **Share a read-only link**.
2. Write who the link is for and how many days it should work (1 to 180).
3. Copy the link or the embed code. They are shown once.

Anyone with the link sees the charts as they stand whenever they open it, without signing in. Finance figures are never shown through a link. **Withdraw** stops a link at once.
