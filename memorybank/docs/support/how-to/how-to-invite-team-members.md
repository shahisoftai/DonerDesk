# How to Invite Team Members

Only **Admins** can invite people and change roles.

## Invite from the Team page

1. Click **Team** in the main menu.
2. Click **Invite member**.
3. Enter the person's **Email** and choose a **Role** (see [Roles and permissions](/support/getting-started/user-roles-and-permissions)).
4. Click **Send invite**.

An **Invitation created** panel shows the acceptance link (`/invite/accept?token=…`). **DonorDesk does not send invitation emails at the moment**, so copy the link, add your workspace's address in front of it, and send it to the person yourself (email, chat, etc.).

## What the invitee does

1. Opens the link (**Join your workspace**).
2. Enters their name and a password (at least 8 characters).
3. Clicks **Accept invitation** and signs in.

They join with the role you chose. Links expire; if it stops working, invite the person again.

## Seats

Full seats (everyone except Viewers) count against your plan: Starter 1, Team 5, Growth 15. Read-only **Viewers** are counted separately (Starter 2, others unlimited). If you are at the limit you must free a seat or upgrade. See [Plans and limits](/support/account-billing/plans-and-limits).

## Assign people to projects

Invited members do not automatically appear on a project. Open the project → **Team** → **Assign member**, choose the member and a **project role**. You can **Remove member** to close the assignment.

## You can also invite during setup

The onboarding checklist has an **Invite your team** step that opens the same form.
