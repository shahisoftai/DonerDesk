# How to Invite Team Members

DonorDesk works best when your whole team is on it. Here is how to invite colleagues to your workspace.

## Before You Start

- You need Owner or Admin role to invite people
- Know the email address of the person you want to invite
- Know what role they should have

## Step-by-Step

### Step 1: Go to Team Settings

1. Click your name or the menu in the top right
2. Select **Team** or go to **Settings → Team**

### Step 2: Click Invite

Click **Invite Team Member** or the **+** button.

### Step 3: Enter Their Details

Fill in:

| Field | What to enter |
|-------|--------------|
| **Email** | Their work email address |
| **Role** | Their role in DonorDesk (see role guide) |
| **Projects** | (Optional) Which projects they can access |

### Step 4: Choose Their Role

Select one of these roles:

| Role | Best for |
|------|---------|
| **Admin** | Organisation managers, directors |
| **Project Manager** | Programme managers, team leads |
| **M&E Officer** | M&E staff, data officers |
| **Field Officer** | Field staff who log activities |
| **Grants Officer** | Staff responsible for donor reporting |
| **Compliance Officer** | Quality assurance, compliance staff |

### Step 5: Send the Invitation

Click **Send Invitation**.

The person will receive an email with a link to join your workspace.

## Project-Level Permissions

When inviting someone, you can also specify which projects they can access:

1. In the invitation form, go to **Projects**
2. Select the projects they should work on
3. They will only see those projects (unless they are an Owner or Admin)

## Managing Existing Team Members

To view all team members:
1. Go to **Team** settings
2. See the full list with their roles, status, and last active date

To change someone's role:
1. Find their name in the team list
2. Click on their role
3. Select the new role
4. Confirm

To remove someone:
1. Click on their name
2. Click **Remove from workspace**
3. Confirm

They will lose access immediately.

## Resending an Invitation

If someone has not received their invitation:

1. Go to **Team** settings
2. Find the pending invitation
3. Click **Resend**

## Role Limits by Plan

| Plan | Maximum team members |
|------|---------------------|
| Starter | 1 (only you) |
| Team | 5 |
| Growth | 15 |
| Enterprise | Unlimited |

## Pending Invitations

If someone has been invited but has not yet accepted, their status shows as "Pending". Pending invitations do not count as team members and do not use your seat quota.

## Best Practices

1. **Invite people with their work email** — Not a shared or personal email
2. **Give the minimum necessary role** — Do not make everyone an Admin
3. **Assign to specific projects** — Only give access to projects they work on
4. **Regularly review** — Check who has access and remove people who have left
