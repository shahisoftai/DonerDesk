# How to Delete Your Account

This guide explains how to delete your DonorDesk account. Note that deleting your account may affect your entire organisation workspace.

## Before You Delete

**Important:** If you are the Owner of your organisation workspace, deleting your account will affect the entire organisation.

Consider these alternatives:

### Instead of Deleting

| Option | What it does |
|--------|-------------|
| **Remove yourself from a project** | Keeps the project, removes only your access |
| **Transfer ownership** | Gives someone else ownership of the workspace |
| **Downgrade your plan** | Keeps your account but reduces features |
| **Archive old projects** | Keeps data but removes from active view |

## How to Delete Your Account

### If You Are Not the Owner

1. Click your name → **Settings**
2. Click **Account Settings** or **Delete Account**
3. Follow the prompts
4. Confirm with your password

This removes your personal account but keeps the organisation and projects intact.

### If You Are the Owner

Deleting the owner account requires transferring ownership first:

**Step 1: Transfer Ownership**
1. Go to **Settings → Team**
2. Find the person you want to make owner
3. Click on their name
4. Click **Transfer Ownership**
5. Confirm

**Step 2: Delete Your Account**
1. Log in as the new owner (or stay logged in if ownership transfer did not fully apply)
2. Go to your personal settings
3. Find **Delete Account**
4. Confirm with your password

## What Happens to Your Data?

| Data type | What happens |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Your user profile** | Permanently deleted |
| **Projects you own** | Must be transferred before deletion |
| **Evidence and reports** | Remain with the organisation |
| **Your activity history** | Anonymised (cannot be linked to you) |
| **Audit logs** | Retained for compliance |

## What Happens to the Organisation?

If you are the Owner and you delete your account:
- The organisation stays active
- Ownership transfers to the person you selected
- You lose all access to the workspace

## Recovering a Deleted Account

Once deleted, your account **cannot be recovered**. You would need to create a new account with the same email, but you would not regain access to the previous organisation.

## How to Transfer Ownership Instead

Transferring ownership keeps the organisation intact while giving someone else admin control:

1. Go to **Settings → Team**
2. Find the team member who should become owner
3. Click **Change Role → Owner**
4. Or click their name → **Transfer Ownership**

## Contacting Support

If you are having trouble deleting your account or transferring ownership:

Email: **support@donordesk.online**

Include:
- Your registered email
- The organisation name
- What you are trying to do

## Data Export Before Deleting

Before deleting your account, you may want to export your data:

1. Go to **Settings → Data Export** (if available)
2. Request an export of your data
3. Download the file
4. Then proceed with deletion

This may not be available on all plans.
