# How to Use the Academy Tour and Demo Project

**DonorDesk Academy** teaches the real reporting workflow using a sample project, so you can learn without touching live data.

## Take the tour

- **Not signed up yet?** The public [product tour](/tour) shows real portal screenshots, step by step, with no account needed.
- **In your workspace:** choose **Take the product tour** on Home or the setup page, or use the **Academy** button in the top bar. It takes about **10–15 minutes**. You can leave and **Resume the product tour** later; progress is remembered.

The tour has ten steps: your sample project, project setup checklist, donor template, logframe and indicators, evidence library, reporting periods, AI-assisted draft, review and refine, compliance checklist and export.

## The demo project

Starting the tour creates a **demo project** in your workspace, a complete sample with a logframe, indicators, a reviewed template and a reporting profile.

- It is clearly marked **Demo**.
- It **does not count** toward your active project limit and **uses no AI credits**. The AI draft step shows pre-generated sample content.
- You can create it again, and **delete it** at any time from its banner. Deleting removes the demo project and everything in it.

## Contextual help

Look for the **Help** buttons on the report editor, templates and compliance screens. They link straight to the relevant support articles, such as *How donor templates work*, *How compliance checks work* and *Report writing tips*.
