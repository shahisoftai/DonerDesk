# Collect answers with a form

A collection form is a set of questions your team answers, or people answer through a link. Accepted answers count into indicator values.

## Design a form

1. Open the project's **Data** tab and choose **Collection forms**, then **New form**.
2. Name it. Add questions and choose each answer type: number, text, date, choice, yes/no, place, GPS point, or photo or document.
3. For a question, you can tick **Always required**, or set **Ask this only when** or **Make it required when** based on an earlier question.
4. Say which indicator the answers add up to: **Count the records**, or **Add up a number** from one question. Choose which date decides the reporting period (otherwise the day it was sent).
5. Choose **Save the draft**, then **Publish** when the questions are right.

A published form cannot be changed. To change questions, close it and make a new form; earlier answers keep the questions they were given.

## Answer a form

Open the form and choose **Fill in**. Answers that do not fit the question are explained beside it. Photos are JPEG, PNG, WebP or PDF, up to 5 MB.

## Import many answers from a sheet

On **Fill in**, choose **Download the sheet**, fill one row per record, then choose a file or paste the rows and choose **Check the sheet**. If a row has a problem, nothing is imported; **Download the rows to fix** gives you only the rows that need correcting. Choose **Import** when the check is clean.

## Review

On **Records**, accept a record or reject it with a reason. **Return to waiting** puts an accepted or rejected record back. Only accepted records count.

On **What it adds up to**, choose a reporting period and **Enter as drafts**. The totals become draft indicator values; review and submit them from the indicator values page.

## Share with people outside the team

On **Share a link**, write who the link is for and choose **Make a link**. Anyone with it can answer the form without signing in. **Withdraw** stops a link.
