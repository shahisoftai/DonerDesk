# How to Create the Closing Report

The **closing report** (a **Final** report) closes your reporting cycle and covers the project's whole life: project-wide figures, finance, evidence and sign-offs. The guided page checks what is ready, tells you what is left and where to do it, and starts the report with the right dates.

## Open it

Go to **Projects → project → Reporting** and click **Closing report**. (The New reporting period page also links to it from the Final option.)

## The steps

Each step shows **Done**, **To do** or **After you start**, a sentence about what is missing and a button that goes to the right place:

1. **Indicator calculations confirmed:** every indicator's calculation is confirmed, so the report can say whether targets were met. See [Understanding indicators and targets](/support/getting-started/indicators-and-targets).
2. **Earlier reports approved:** your regular reports (Monthly to Annual) are approved or submitted. Activity, Situation and Custom reports never block this.
3. **Project-wide figures complete:** each indicator that adds up over time has a baseline, a target and a verified cumulative value. Rates and ratios are not reported cumulatively.
4. **Finance reported:** if finance is switched on, enter and verify the final figures. This step reads **After you start**, because finance is entered on the report itself.
5. **Activities accepted:** the closing report rolls up accepted activity records.
6. **Donor template approved:** or the built-in closing structure is used.
7. **Sign-offs assigned:** a project manager and an M&E officer are assigned to review and approve.

Steps still to do do not stop you starting; you can start and finish them as you go.

## Start it

When the project can take a closing report, the page shows the dates it will cover (from the end of your last regular period to the project's end) and **Start the closing report**. It creates the Final period through the same checks as any other period and builds its checklist, then opens it.

## When you can't start

The page says why. Common reasons: a closing report already exists (a link takes you to it), the project is **Completed** or **Archived**, or every part of the project's dates already has a period. A one-off over existing dates is a **Custom** report.
