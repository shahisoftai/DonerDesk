# How to Make an Infographic

An infographic is a one-page (or multi-page) picture of your results. Every figure in it comes from your project's records, so you do not type numbers into it.

## Start one

1. Open the project and go to **Infographics**.
2. Click **New infographic**.
3. Choose a template. Each one suits a different use: a humanitarian snapshot, a response at a glance, a funding snapshot, a donor one-pager, a social card and others. A template is only a starting point; you can change every block.

## Build it

- Click a block in the list, or click it on the page, to edit it.
- Use **Undo** and **Redo** (Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z) while you work.
- Drag a block to move it. Its width is set in the block's own panel.
- A block with no reported data says **Not yet reported** or **Not measured**. It never shows a 0 in place of a missing value.

## Choose the page

- **Format:** A4, A3, square, portrait 4:5, story 9:16, slide 16:9 or a 3:1 web banner. Pick the format before you lay out the blocks, because the page changes shape.
- **Theme:** five built-in themes, including a high-contrast theme and a greyscale theme that prints well. If your organisation has a brand kit, its theme is also offered.
- **Language:** English, French, Spanish or Arabic. Arabic is set right to left. Maps and charts keep their direction.

## Save and look again

Your design saves as you work. The **Design** panel has the **Size**, **Theme** and **Language of the fixed words** settings. The preview on the right is drawn by the same code that makes the published file, so what you see is what you publish.
