# How to Use the Activity Feed

The Activity Feed provides a real-time timeline of all actions taken on donor records, ensuring transparency and supporting audit compliance.

## Accessing the Activity Feed

The Activity Feed appears on:
- Each donor profile — showing all activity for that donor
- The main dashboard — showing recent activity across your organisation

## What Gets Logged

Every action is recorded, including:
- Donation made, failed, or refunded
- Donor profile created or updated
- Note added to a record
- Tag added or removed
- Email sent to donor
- Team member logged in
- Payment method added or removed
- Campaign created or modified

## Reading the Activity Feed

Each entry shows:
- **Action** — what happened (e.g., "Donation received")
- **Details** — specifics (e.g., "$100 via credit card")
- **Actor** — who performed the action (team member or donor self-service)
- **Timestamp** — when it occurred

## Filtering the Activity Feed

On the dashboard feed:
- Filter by action type
- Filter by team member
- Filter by date range
- Search by donor name

## Activity on Donor Profiles

On individual donor profiles, the Activity Feed tab shows:
- Complete history of interactions
- Donations with amounts and dates
- Notes and communications
- Profile changes

Click any entry to expand details or navigate to the related record.

## Exporting Activity Data

To export activity for compliance:
1. Go to **Settings > Audit & Compliance**
2. Click **Export Activity Log**
3. Set date range
4. Choose format (CSV or JSON)
5. Download

## Retention Policy

Activity logs are retained based on your plan:
- Starter: 1 year
- Professional: 3 years
- Enterprise: 7 years or custom

For help with the activity feed, contact support@donordesk.online.
