# How to Choose the Areas a Project Works In (Where we work)

**Where we work** is the one page where a project says which places it works in. Everything that shows a place (maps, results by place, the work plan's location box) links back to it. Open it from **Projects → your project → Plan → Where we work**.

Your organisation keeps one shared list of places (**Settings → Places**, see [How to set up your places list](/support/how-to/set-up-your-places-list)). This page is the project's own view of it: you tick the areas this project works in and they go straight into that list, spelled the way the maps expect.

## Areas: tick where you work

1. Open the **Areas** tab. Under **Country**, choose the country (it starts on the project's own country).
2. A list of its first-level areas (states or provinces) appears beside a map. Tick an area to include it. Ticking an area includes everything inside it.
3. Click **▸** beside an area to open the areas inside it (districts, then sub-districts where boundary files exist) and tick those instead if the project works in only some of them.
4. Click an area on the map to see its name; the buttons next to it tick it or look inside. A line under the list says how many areas of the country are already in your places list (for example *4 areas of Ethiopia are in your places list*).

Nothing else is needed: the ticks are saved in the places list at once. To remove an area, open **Settings → Places** and retire it there, because another project may use it.

## Points: single places

The **Points** tab is for places that are not areas on the map: a camp, a clinic, a school or a village. Add each with a name, what it is, the place it is **Inside**, and, if you know it, a pin (latitude and longitude, or place the pin on the map). A pin lets DonorDesk draw it on maps and measure distances.

## To confirm

The **To confirm (n)** tab lists words your team typed as a location that no place in the list answers to yet, most used first. Add the ones that are real places (they then match everywhere), or fix the spelling where it was typed. If a name matches more than one place, add where it is, for example *Rabak, White Nile*.

## Who can do this

People who can edit projects can tick areas and add points. Everyone else can see the page. See [How to record a figure for each place](/support/how-to/record-figures-by-place) to get figures onto the map, and [How to see your work on a map](/support/how-to/see-your-work-on-a-map).
