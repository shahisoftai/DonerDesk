# How to Choose the Areas a Project Works In (Where we work)

**Where we work** is the page where a project says which areas it works in and sees the places its work happened. Open it from **Projects → your project → Plan → Where we work**. Only this project changes here: other projects keep their own areas.

Your organisation has one shared list of places. A village or a district is entered once and used by every project, so the organisation can see when two projects work in the same place. The organisation's own view is **Home → Where we work** (see below).

## Areas: tick where this project works

1. Open the **Areas** tab. Under **Country**, choose the country (it starts on the project's own country).
2. A list of its first-level areas (states or provinces) appears beside a map. Tick an area to add it to this project. Ticking an area includes everything inside it.
3. Click **▸** beside an area to open the areas inside it and tick those instead if the project works in only some of them. Ticking a larger area replaces the smaller ones of this project inside it.
4. A line under the list says how many areas the project works in (for example *This project works in 4 areas of Ethiopia*).

To take an area out, untick it. This is refused while the project has records in that area (planned or completed activities, figures by place, files or people reached): the message says how many, and the area's **Close…** button opens. **Close** asks why the project stopped working there. A closed area keeps all its history and still counts in results and maps; it is simply no longer offered for new work. **Reopen** brings it back.

## Sites

The **Sites** tab lists the places this project's records point at (villages, clinics, schools), with what was recorded at each. Places outside the project's areas are listed first, with a button to add the area they are in. **Add a site** adds a new place inside one of the project's areas.

## To confirm

The **To confirm (n)** tab lists words typed on this project as a location that no place answers to yet, most used first. Choose the place each word means (a suggestion, or **Choose the place…**). Every record of the project with that word then points at the place, and the word leaves the list. Nothing typed is changed.

## Choosing places on records

On a planned activity, a completed activity or a file, **Places** shows where it happened. A completed activity takes the places of its planned activity, and a file the places of its activity, until you change them. When you search, this project's places come first; **Add a new place** adds one inside one of the project's areas without leaving the page.

## The organisation's view

**Home → Where we work** shows, for each province, district or site, which projects work there and how many activities were planned and done. Click a place for its own page. People with the Admin role use **Manage places** there to merge duplicate places, retire places, import a list and set map layers.

## Who can do this

People who can edit projects can tick, close and reopen areas and confirm words. People who record work can choose places on their own records and add sites. Outside partners can only choose and add places inside the project's areas.
