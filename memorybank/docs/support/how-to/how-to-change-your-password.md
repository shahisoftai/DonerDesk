# How to Change Your Password

Keeping your password strong and secure is important. Here is how to change it in DonorDesk.

## Step-by-Step

### Step 1: Open Settings

1. Click your name in the top right corner
2. Click **Settings**

### Step 2: Go to Security

1. In Settings, click **Security**
2. You will see your current password field

### Step 3: Enter Current Password

1. Type your **current password** in the first field
2. This confirms your identity

### Step 4: Choose a New Password

1. Type your **new password** in the second field
2. Type it again in the third field to **confirm**

### Step 5: Save

Click **Update Password** or **Save**.

You will see a confirmation message.

### Step 6: Log In Again

For security, you may be asked to log in again with your new password.

## Password Requirements

Your new password must:
- Be at least **8 characters** long
- Include at least one **uppercase letter**
- Include at least one **lowercase letter**
- Include at least one **number**
- Include at least one **special character** (!@#$%^&* etc.)

## Tips for a Strong Password

**Good password examples:**
- `Nourishment2026!Secure`
- `Health#Project$2026`
- `M&E!Team@Work2026`

**Bad password examples (too easy to guess):**
- `password123`
- `donordesk`
- `12345678`
- `your name`

## What to Do If You Forget Your Password

If you cannot log in because you forgot your password:

1. Go to the **login page**
2. Click **Forgot password**
3. Enter your **email address**
4. Check your inbox (and spam folder)
5. Click the **reset link** in the email
6. Choose a new password

## How Often Should You Change Your Password?

- Change your password if you think it has been compromised
- Change it if you shared it with someone accidentally
- There is no mandatory rotation — only change it when needed

## What To Do If Someone Else Knows Your Password

If you suspect someone has your password:

1. **Change your password immediately**
2. Check your account for any unauthorised changes
3. Contact **support@donordesk.online** if you see suspicious activity

## Sharing Passwords — Don't!

Never share your password with colleagues. Instead:
- Invite them as team members so they have their own access
- Share access through proper DonorDesk permissions
- If someone needs temporary access, create a temporary account

## Password Managers

Consider using a password manager to:
- Generate strong random passwords
- Store passwords securely
- Auto-fill login forms

Popular password managers:
- Bitwarden (free, open source)
- 1Password
- LastPass
- Google Password Manager (in Chrome)

## Two-Factor Authentication (Coming Soon)

When two-factor authentication (2FA) is available, we strongly recommend enabling it. 2FA requires both your password and a code from your phone to log in, making your account much more secure.
