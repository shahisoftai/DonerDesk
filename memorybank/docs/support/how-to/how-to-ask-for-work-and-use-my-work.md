# How to Ask for Work and Use the Inbox

The **Inbox** (the second entry in the left menu; it replaces the old **My Work** page) is the one list of everything that is yours, by when it is due: planned activities you lead, requests people have sent you, checklist items, report deadlines, files to review and values to enter.

## See what is yours

Open **Inbox** in the left menu. Its **To do** tab opens first. Late items come first, then this week, then later; the project buttons above the list narrow it to one project. Field Officers also see their week on **Home**. Choose an item to open it.

## Ask someone to do something

- **Ask for completed activity** – on the Inbox's **Team** tab, for the project you are looking at. Pick which planned activity it is about, then who should enter what happened.
- **Ask for a file** – open the planned activity and choose the button.
- **Ask for this value** (on an indicator page) – ask the person who collects it.
- **Ask someone…** – on the Inbox's **Team** tab, for anything else, in your own words.

Choose **Who**, check **By when**, add a note if it helps, tick **Urgent** only when it cannot wait, and select **Send**. The person gets a note in the bell and sees it in their Inbox. A request closes by itself when the work is done: you do not need to tick it off.

You can only ask people who are allowed to do the work (for example, someone who can enter completed activities).

## See your team's work

Project Managers and M&E Officers have a **Team** tab in the Inbox showing each person's open and late work across a project, so you can see who needs help before anything is late. Inside a project, the same workload is under **Settings → Team**.

## Change who leads

On a planned activity, indicator or report, choose **Change lead**. A lead can be any active member of the project.
