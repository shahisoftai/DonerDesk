# How to Work Offline and Take Photos

Field staff often record activities where the signal is poor. DonorDesk keeps what you finish and sends it when you are back online.

## Take a photo as evidence

On a phone, the activity form and the evidence upload form have a **Take a photo** button. It opens the camera; the photo is added to the files to upload. Each photo is named from the time it was taken (for example *photo-20270312-141502.jpg*), so several photos in a row never replace each other. On a computer the button opens the image chooser instead.

## Finish an activity with no connection

1. Fill in the **New activity** form as usual, add photos, and click **Submit activity**.
2. With no connection, the page says **Saved on this device** and shows the activity's title. You can leave the page.
3. A bar at the bottom of the screen shows *1 record is saved on this device and will be sent when you're back online.*
4. When the connection returns, the activity is created and its photos are uploaded automatically. If the connection drops half-way, only what is missing is sent next time; nothing is created twice. You can also click **Send now**.

Click **Record another activity** to keep going while still offline.

## If a saved record cannot be sent

If the server refuses a record (for example a required field is missing), it moves to **needs your attention** with the reason. Click **Try again** after fixing the cause, or **Discard** to delete it from the device. If only some files were refused, the activity itself is saved and the message names the files.

## What to know

- Only the **new activity form** (with its photos) works offline. Other forms need a connection and show *You appear to be offline* so you can try again.
- Saved records stay on **that device and browser, for the person signed in**. On a shared phone, one person's records are never sent under another person's login.
- Clearing the browser's site data before the records are sent deletes them. Stay signed in until the bar disappears.
- The page itself must already be open (or loaded) to be used without a signal; opening DonorDesk for the first time offline is not supported yet.
- On a phone, use your browser's **Add to Home screen** to open DonorDesk like an app.
