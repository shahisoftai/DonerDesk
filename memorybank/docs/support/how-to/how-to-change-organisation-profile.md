# How to Change Your Organisation Profile

Go to **Settings → Settings**. Admins can edit; other roles see the page read-only ("You do not have permission to edit organization settings").

## What you can edit

- **Organization name**
- **Organization type** – local NGO, national NGO, international NGO, UN implementing partner, consulting firm, government unit, other
- **Country** and **Main office location**
- **Sectors**
- **Contact name** and **Contact email**
- **Website**
- **Donor types served**
- **Default language**
- **Data residency** – platform default, EU, US, Africa or Asia
- **AI-assisted reporting** – the **AI enabled** switch. When off, generating a report creates a blank draft for manual writing. Turning AI off never blocks manual editing.

Click **Save settings**.

## Related settings

- **Default reporting profile** (language, tone, rules for new projects) is in **Settings → Setup**.
- **AI Writing Style** (learn from your team's edits) appears as its own tab when enabled for your workspace. See [AI Settings](/support/advanced-features/ai-settings).
- **Storage** – reconnect or change in **Settings → Setup**.

Changes are recorded in the audit log.
