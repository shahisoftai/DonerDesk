# How to Change Your Organisation Profile

Your organisation profile is your NGO or programme's public-facing identity in DonorDesk. Here is how to update it.

## What Is the Organisation Profile?

The organisation profile stores:
- Your organisation's name
- Type (Local NGO, INGO, Government programme, etc.)
- Country
- Sectors you work in
- Contact information
- Logo
- Website

This information appears on reports and helps your team identify their workspace.

## Step-by-Step

### Step 1: Open Settings

1. Click your name in the top right corner
2. Click **Settings**

### Step 2: Go to Organisation Profile

1. In Settings, click **Organisation Profile** or **General**
2. You will see the current profile information

### Step 3: Edit the Fields

| Field | What to enter |
|-------|--------------|
| **Organisation name** | Your full official name |
| **Organisation type** | Select from: Local NGO, National NGO, INGO, UN implementing partner, Consulting firm, Government programme unit, Other |
| **Country** | Select your country |
| **Sectors** | Select all sectors you work in (Nutrition, Health, WASH, etc.) |
| **Contact name** | Primary contact person |
| **Contact email** | Primary contact email |
| **Website** | Your organisation's website (optional) |
| **Logo** | Upload your logo image (optional) |

### Step 4: Save

Click **Save Changes**.

## Who Can Edit the Organisation Profile?

Only these roles can edit the organisation profile:
- **Owner**
- **Admin**

If you do not see the option to edit, ask your Owner or Admin.

## Uploading Your Logo

1. In the Logo section, click **Upload**
2. Select your logo file (JPG, PNG, or SVG)
3. The logo will be saved and displayed on your profile

**Logo tips:**
- Use a square or near-square image
- Minimum 200x200 pixels recommended
- File size under 2 MB

## Sectors

Select all the sectors your organisation works in. You can select multiple sectors:
- Nutrition
- Food Security
- Health
- WASH
- Protection
- Education
- Livelihoods
- Shelter
- Multi-sector
- Other

This helps DonorDesk understand your work and may improve AI report generation.

## What Happens to the Logo?

Your logo appears on:
- Your DonorDesk dashboard header
- Exported reports (if configured)
- Your organisation's public profile page

## Changing Organisation Name

The organisation name is visible to all team members and appears on reports. Choose a name your team will recognise.

## What Cannot Be Changed

Some fields may be locked depending on your subscription:
- Organisation type may require admin approval
- Country cannot be changed after a certain point

Contact support if you need to change a locked field.

## Organisation Settings vs User Settings

**Organisation settings** (this page):
- Affects the whole organisation
- Only Owner/Admin can change
- Includes: name, type, sectors, logo

**User settings** (your personal account):
- Only affects you
- Change from your name → Settings
- Includes: your name, email, password
