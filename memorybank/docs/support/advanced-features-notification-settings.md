# Advanced: Configuring Notification Settings

DonorDesk can send you notifications when important things happen. Here is how to set them up.

## Accessing Notification Settings

1. Click your name → **Settings**
2. Click **Notifications** or **Notification Preferences**

## Types of Notifications

### Report Notifications

| Notification | When you get it |
|-------------|----------------|
| Report due reminder | 7 days and 1 day before deadline |
| Report submitted | When a report is submitted for review |
| Report approved | When a report is approved |
| Report rejected | When a report needs changes |

### Evidence Notifications

| Notification | When you get it |
|-------------|----------------|
| Evidence uploaded | A team member uploads evidence |
| Evidence verified | Evidence you uploaded is verified |
| Evidence needs correction | Evidence requires changes |

### Compliance Notifications

| Notification | When you get it |
|-------------|----------------|
| Critical item opened | A critical compliance issue is found |
| Item assigned to you | A compliance item is assigned |
| Item due soon | A compliance item is approaching its due date |
| Item overdue | A compliance item has passed its due date |

### Team Notifications

| Notification | When you get it |
|-------------|----------------|
| Team member invited | Someone is invited to the workspace |
| Role changed | Your role or someone else is changed |

### Project Notifications

| Notification | When you get it |
|-------------|----------------|
| Project created | A new project is created |
| Project status changed | A project becomes active, paused, etc. |

## Setting Up Notification Channels

### Email Notifications

1. Find **Email Notifications**
2. Toggle on the notifications you want to receive by email
3. Some notifications are always sent (e.g., invitation emails)

### In-App Notifications

These appear in DonorDesk when you log in:
1. Find **In-App Notifications**
2. Toggle on what you want to see

### Browser Notifications

If supported, you can get desktop notifications:
1. Enable in your browser's notification settings
2. Allow DonorDesk to send notifications

## Notification Frequency

For some notifications, you can choose frequency:

| Option | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Immediate** | Sent right away |
| **Daily digest** | Combined into one email per day |
| **Weekly digest** | Combined into one email per week |
| **Off** | No notification |

## Do Not Disturb

### Quiet Hours

Set times when you do not want to receive notifications:
1. Find **Do Not Disturb** or **Quiet Hours**
2. Set the start time (e.g., 6 PM)
3. Set the end time (e.g., 9 AM)
4. Notifications during this time are held

## Notification Permissions

Some notifications are mandatory and cannot be turned off:
- Security notifications (unusual login)
- Billing notifications (payment issues)
- Invitation emails (for the person invited)

## Managing Notifications for Your Role

### As an Admin
You receive notifications about:
- All projects in the organisation
- Team management actions
- Compliance issues across all projects

### As a Project Manager
You receive notifications about:
- Your assigned projects
- Reports and compliance in those projects
- Team activity on your projects

### As an M&E Officer
You receive notifications about:
- Evidence uploads and verification status
- Indicator updates
- Compliance items assigned to you

## Troubleshooting Missing Notifications

### Check Your Email

1. Check the email address is correct
2. Check your spam/junk folder
3. Add donor-desk.online to your email whitelist

### Check Notification Settings

1. Go to Settings → Notifications
2. Make sure the notification is toggled ON
3. Check the channel (email, in-app, browser)

### Check Your Role

Some notifications are role-specific. If you do not see a notification:
1. Check if your role should receive it
2. Ask your admin if you need a different role

## Notification Preferences by Role

### M&E Officer Recommended Settings

- Evidence uploaded: ON
- Evidence verified: ON
- Indicator updates: ON
- Compliance items assigned: ON

### Project Manager Recommended Settings

- Report due reminder: ON
- Report submitted: ON
- Compliance critical: ON
- Team activity: ON

### Admin Recommended Settings

- All notifications: ON (or review daily digest)
- Team management: ON
- Security: ON
- Billing: ON
