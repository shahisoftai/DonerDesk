# Enter results in the sheet

On a reporting period's indicator values page you can switch between the **Form** and the **Sheet**. The Sheet shows every indicator as a row and the values as columns, like a spreadsheet. Both save to the same place, so you can switch at any time.

## Type and move

Click a cell and type. Use the arrow keys, Tab and Enter to move; F2 edits the cell you are on, Esc cancels, and Delete clears. Use Shift with the arrow keys to select a block, and drag the corner handle to fill down.

## Paste from Excel or Google Sheets

Copy a block of cells and paste into the Sheet. The columns **This period, Cumulative, Notes** and **Data source** are side by side, so a four-column block lands exactly. A value that is not possible (for example, text in a number column) is named and not stored.

## See what is saved

A small mark and a label on each cell tell you its state: not saved, saved, locked, calculated, or not applicable. Verified values, calculated values and values in a closed period are locked and are never changed by pasting, filling or clearing.

Drafts are saved for you a few seconds after you stop typing, and only while every value is possible. Nothing is handed in or verified by saving. If you lose your connection, your changes wait on your device and are sent when you are back online.

## Find your way around

Use **Ctrl+F** to find a row and **Ctrl+G** to go to one. You can hide, move and resize columns, change the row height and zoom, filter by state, sort, and show earlier periods beside the current one. Choose **History of this value** on a cell to see who changed its value and when.

## Use a spreadsheet file

Choose **Download Excel** for a workbook with the checks built in (numbers, 0 to 100, Yes or No) and **Import Excel** to bring it back. The imported values are put on the sheet for you to review, then save. Rows are matched by the hidden id column, or by code. Rows that cannot be matched and locked rows are listed and left alone.

## Not applicable

If an indicator does not apply this period, choose **Not applicable** and give a short reason. It is an answer, not a gap: reports do not list it as missing.
