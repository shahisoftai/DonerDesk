# Edit a dashboard template

Templates are always drafts you can change. There is no approval step.

## Make your own copy

The built-in templates cannot be changed in place. Open **Dashboards**, then **Start from a sector template**, choose a built-in template and choose **Copy and edit**. The copy belongs to your organisation and shows who it is based on.

## What you can change

- The name, the sector and the description.
- The **roles** (the kinds of indicator the template asks for). A role that charts use cannot be removed until those charts are removed or changed; the page names them.
- The **charts**: add one, change its type or what it shows, move it, resize it or remove it. Text notes can be added as well.

Each change is checked as a whole, and the page tells you in a sentence what is wrong if something cannot be saved. If someone else saved the template while you were editing, your save is refused and nothing is merged; reload and make your change again.

## Delete

You can delete a template your organisation owns. Dashboards already made from it are not affected.
