# Build and save a report

The report builder asks one question across several indicators and gives you a table. Open a project, choose **Data**, then **Report builder**.

## Ask the question

The question reads as a sentence: *Showing results for [indicators] from [period] to [period]; display [what] by [rows], broken down by [columns].*

1. Tick the indicators.
2. Choose the first and last period (or leave them to take every period).
3. Choose what to display: the value for each period, the cumulative value to date, or the percent of target.
4. Choose how to lay it out: indicators down and periods across, periods down and indicators across, or indicators broken down by a group (sex, age group, disability, location or other group). A group breakdown shows the groups for the last period you chose, with the total that was recorded beside them.
5. Choose whether to include values that are not verified yet. Choose **Run report**.

The sentence under the form always repeats what you chose. A gap is shown as "Not measured" and a real zero as 0. Different units are never added together, and a rate is never judged as a percent of target. Notes under the table say how many figures are not verified yet.

## Keep it

- **Download CSV** gives you the table as a spreadsheet file.
- Give the report a name and choose **Save as new**. Keep it for yourself, or share it with the project (this needs someone who manages projects). A saved report keeps the question, not the figures, so it is run again each time you open it.
- Choose a dashboard and **Add to a dashboard** to show the table there; it updates each time the dashboard is opened.
