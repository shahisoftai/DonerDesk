# Bookmark pages

Use the star in the header to keep a shortcut to the page you are on: a chart, a map, a report, an indicator, a dashboard, or any other page.

1. Open the page and choose **Bookmark**. The star fills and reads **Bookmarked**.
2. Your bookmarks are listed on your Home page. Choose one to open it, or the cross beside it to remove it.
3. To remove the bookmark for the page you are on, choose **Bookmarked** again.

You can keep up to 30 bookmarks. A bookmark is only a shortcut and is only yours: opening it shows the page as it is today, with the access you have today. Nobody else can see your bookmarks.
