# How to Use the Audit Trail

The audit log records who did what and when: project changes, imports, evidence uploads and verification decisions, report edits and approvals, exports, role changes, billing changes and more. It is your proof of process for donors and auditors.

## Open it

Go to **Settings → Audit log**. It is available to Admin, Project Manager and Compliance Officer.

## Filter

- **Actor** – all actors or one person
- **Event** – all events or one type
- **Entity ID / Filter by entity** – everything that happened to one record
- Use the pagination controls to move through results.

## What each record shows

The time, the person, the event, the record affected and the details. Personal or sensitive values are redacted in the view.

## Useful for

- Showing a donor exactly how a figure was verified and approved.
- Investigating an unexpected change.
- Checking who exported a report and which version.
- Reviewing role changes.

Audit records cannot be edited or deleted. For details see [Understanding audit logs](/support/getting-started/understanding-audit-logs).
