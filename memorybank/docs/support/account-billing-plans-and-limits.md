# Understanding Billing Plans and Limits

## Plan Limits Overview

Your DonorDesk plan sets limits on three key resources:

| Resource | Starter (Free) | Team ($59/mo) | Growth ($149/mo) | Enterprise |
|---------|--------------|----------------|-------------------|------------|
| Active projects | 1 | 5 | 20 | Unlimited |
| Team members | 1 | 5 | 15 | Unlimited |
| Managed storage | 1 GB | 25 GB | 100 GB | Custom |
| AI drafts/month | 5 | 100 | 500 | Unlimited |

## What Happens When You Hit a Limit

### Projects

If you are at your project limit and try to create another:
- You see a warning that you have reached your limit
- You can either:
  - Archive or delete an existing project (frees up the slot)
  - Upgrade to a higher plan

Archived projects are read-only but do not count toward your limit.

### Team Members

If you are at your seat limit:
- Pending invitations still work (they do not count as members until accepted)
- To add a new member, either:
  - Remove an existing member
  - Upgrade your plan

### Storage

If you hit your storage limit:
- New uploads are blocked
- To free space:
  - Delete old evidence files
  - Delete old exports
  - Switch to linking files from Google Drive (uses no quota)

### AI Credits

AI credits reset on the 1st of each month. If you run out:
- AI generation is paused until the 1st
- You can still write reports manually
- Or upgrade to a plan with more credits

## Upgrade Mid-Month

When you upgrade mid-month:
- You are charged the prorated difference for the remaining days
- New limits apply immediately
- Your unused credits from the old plan do not carry over

## Downgrade

When you downgrade:
- The change takes effect at the end of your billing period
- Your current features remain until then
- If you have more projects/members than the lower plan allows, you must reduce them before the downgrade date

## Annual vs Monthly Pricing

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Annual savings |
|------|---------|--------|----------------|
| Team | $59 | $590 | $118 (2 months free) |
| Growth | $149 | $1,490 | $298 (2 months free) |

Annual billing is billed once per year. You can cancel before the year ends.

## Enterprise Custom Limits

For Enterprise plans, limits are set in a contract. Contact DonorDesk to negotiate:
- Number of projects
- Number of users
- Storage amount
- AI credit volume
- Any other custom requirements

Enterprise also includes:
- SSO/SAML integration
- SCIM for automated user provisioning
- Custom data residency
- Dedicated support
- SLA guarantee
