# How to Ask for Work and Use My Work

**My Work** is the one list of everything that is yours, by when it is due: planned activities you lead, requests people have sent you, checklist items, report deadlines, files to review and values to enter.

## See what is yours

Open **My Work** in the sidebar. Late items come first, then this week, then later. Field Officers also see their week on **Home**. Choose an item to open it.

## Ask someone to do something

Open the planned activity (or the indicator) and choose one of the buttons:

- **Ask for completed activity** – ask the lead to enter what happened.
- **Ask for a file** – ask for a photo, an attendance sheet or another kind of file.
- **Ask for this value** (on an indicator page) – ask the person who collects it.

Choose **Who**, check **By when**, add a note if it helps, tick **Urgent** only when it cannot wait, and select **Send**. The person gets a note in the bell and sees it in My Work. A request closes by itself when the work is done: you do not need to tick it off.

You can only ask people who are allowed to do the work (for example, someone who can enter completed activities).

## See your team's work

Project Managers and M&E Officers have a **Team** tab on the project showing each person's open and late work, so you can see who needs help before anything is late.

## Change who leads

On a planned activity, indicator or report, choose **Change lead**. A lead can be any active member of the project.
