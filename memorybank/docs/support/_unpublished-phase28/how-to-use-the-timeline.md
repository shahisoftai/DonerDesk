# How to Use the Timeline

The Timeline shows planned activities as bars on a calendar, grouped by output, so you can see overlaps and gaps at a glance. Open **Activities**, choose **Planned**, then **Timeline**. On a phone the Timeline is not shown; you get the List instead.

## Read it

- Each bar is a planned activity. Its colour shows the signal (on time, late, done), and the signal is also in the bar's label.
- A dashed outline shows where an activity was *before* it was moved.
- A darker fill shows progress when you planned a number of sessions.
- A diamond is a milestone (one date).
- The line is today. Triangles along the top are your reports' due dates and internal review dates.
- Choose **Month**, **Quarter** or **Whole project** to change the window. DonorDesk remembers your choice.

## Move an activity

If you are allowed to move it (see below):

1. Drag the bar to move it, or drag either end to change the start or end date.
2. A bar appears: **"Move to 12–14 Mar?"** with **Save** and **Undo**. Nothing is saved until you select Save.
3. If it repeats, choose **Only this one** or **This and later ones**.
4. If the activity's end date has passed, or a report covers it, you must say **Why?** before saving.

**With the keyboard:** select a bar, then press ← or → to move it a day, Shift+← or Shift+→ to change its end date, and Save to confirm. Esc cancels.

Done and cancelled activities cannot be dragged. If you cannot move an activity, its bar has no drag handles.

## Who can move activities

Admins, Project Managers and M&E Officers. A lead who is not one of those can change an activity's status and notes, but not its dates.
