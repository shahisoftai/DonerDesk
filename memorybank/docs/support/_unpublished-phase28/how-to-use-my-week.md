# How to Use My Week

If you are a Field Officer with planned activities, **My week** is at the top of your Home page. It shows only your own work: activities you lead or help with.

## What you see

- **Late**: activities that should already have started or finished.
- **This week** and **Next week**.
- A box for **completed activities to fix**, if a reviewer sent any back to you, with a link to each.
- **Add completed activity**, ready when you have finished something. If you work on several projects, choose the project first.

Choose **List**, **Week** or **Month** to see the same work as a list or on a calendar. The month calendar needs a wider screen.

## Update an activity

Use the status buttons on each row: **Not started**, **Under way**, **Done**. If DonorDesk offers **Mark activity done?** because you have entered completed activities for it, one tap confirms.

## Without a connection

If you change a status while offline, it is kept on your device ("Saved on this device. It will be sent when you are back online.") and sent when the connection returns. If the activity was changed by someone else in the meantime and your change no longer fits, it is set aside for you to look at; it is never sent twice or forced through.

Completed activities you finish offline are saved and sent in the same way.

## If you do not see My week

It appears only when you have planned activities, and only if work planning is shown for the project. Ask your Project Manager.
