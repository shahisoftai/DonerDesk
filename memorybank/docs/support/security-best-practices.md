# Security Best Practices

This guide helps you use DonorDesk securely and protect your organisation's data.

## Password Security

### Creating a Strong Password

Your DonorDesk password should be:
- At least **12 characters** long
- A mix of **uppercase and lowercase** letters
- Include **numbers**
- Include **special characters** (!@#$% etc.)
- Not based on dictionary words or personal information

**Example strong password:** `Nourishment2026!Secure`

**Example weak password:** `password123`

### Changing Your Password

1. Go to **Settings → Security**
2. Click **Change Password**
3. Enter your current password
4. Enter your new password (twice)
5. Click **Save**

### Password Do's and Don'ts

✓ **DO:**
- Use a unique password for DonorDesk
- Use a password manager (Bitwarden, 1Password, etc.)
- Change your password if you suspect it has been compromised

✗ **DON'T:**
- Share your password with anyone
- Use the same password as other services
- Write passwords on paper or in notes apps
- Send passwords via email or chat

## Session Management

### Logging Out

Always log out when using a shared or public device:
1. Click your name in the top right
2. Click **Log out**

### Session Timeout

For security, DonorDesk sessions expire after a period of inactivity. When you return, simply log in again.

On trusted devices, you can check **Remember me** to stay logged in for 30 days.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Two-factor authentication adds a second check when you log in — even if someone knows your password, they cannot get in without your phone.

When 2FA is enabled on your workspace:
1. You will be prompted to set it up on your next login
2. Download an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, Microsoft Authenticator)
3. Scan the QR code
4. Enter the code from the app to confirm

## Sharing and Collaboration

### When to Share Login Details — Don't!

Never share your personal DonorDesk login with a colleague. Each person should have their own account.

**Why?** Because:
- Audit logs track actions by individual user
- If something goes wrong, we can identify who did it
- Shared accounts create security risks
- Permissions are tied to individual accounts

### Inviting Team Members

Instead of sharing passwords, invite your colleagues as team members:
1. Go to **Settings → Team → Invite**
2. Enter their email
3. Assign their role
4. They will receive their own login

## Handling Sensitive Evidence

When uploading files marked as Sensitive or Highly Sensitive:

1. **Mark the correct level** — Do not under-classify sensitive files
2. **Check before exporting** — Verify that sensitive files are not accidentally included in donor submissions
3. **Limit access** — Only share these files with people who genuinely need them
4. **Delete when no longer needed** — Remove old sensitive files from the library

## Phishing and Scams

DonorDesk will never:
- Ask for your password via email
- Ask you to click a link in an email to "verify your account"
- Ask for credit card details via email

If you receive a suspicious email claiming to be from DonorDesk:
1. Do not click any links
2. Forward the email to **support@donordesk.online**
3. Delete the email

## Device Security

### On Public or Shared Devices

- Always log out when finished
- Do not save your password in the browser
- Clear the browser cache after logging out
- Avoid accessing highly sensitive evidence on public computers

### On Personal Devices

- Keep your operating system and browser updated
- Use a reputable antivirus program
- Lock your device when not in use

## If You Suspect a Security Issue

If you believe your account has been compromised or there is a security vulnerability:

**Contact us immediately:**
- Email: **support@donordesk.online**
- Include as much detail as possible
- We will respond within 24 hours

For urgent issues, mark the subject as "URGENT — Security."
