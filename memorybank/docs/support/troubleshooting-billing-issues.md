# Troubleshooting Billing and Payment Issues

## Payment Failed

### "Card declined"

**Solutions:**
1. Check that your card details are correct
2. Make sure the card has not expired
3. Check if the card has sufficient funds
4. Try a different card
5. Contact your bank — they may be blocking the transaction

### "Payment method declined"

Your payment method (card) was declined by the payment processor.

**Solutions:**
1. Try a different card
2. Try PayPal if available
3. Contact **support@donordesk.online** for alternative payment methods

## Unexpected Charge

If you see a charge you do not recognise:
1. Check your invoice history in **Settings → Billing → Invoice History**
2. Make sure the charge is for the correct plan and period
3. Contact **support@donordesk.online** with:
   - Your registered email
   - The amount charged
   - The date of the charge

## Upgrade Not Applied

After payment, your plan should upgrade immediately.

If it does not:
1. Log out and log back in
2. Check **Settings → Billing** to confirm the new plan
3. If still not showing, contact support with your payment confirmation

## Invoice Is Wrong

If your invoice shows incorrect details:
1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Invoice History**
2. Click on the invoice
3. Check the billing address, organisation name, and amount
4. Contact **support@donordesk.online** to request a correction

## Downgrade Not Applied

Your plan should downgrade at the end of your billing period.

If you see your old plan features disappearing immediately:
1. Check the downgrade date in **Settings → Billing**
2. If it applied early by mistake, contact support

## Cannot Find Invoice

1. Check **Settings → Billing → Invoice History**
2. Invoices are also sent to your registered email address
3. If you cannot find it, contact **support@donordesk.online**

## Currency or Tax Is Wrong

Tax is calculated based on your organisation's country and the billing information provided during checkout.

To update billing details:
1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Billing Information**
2. Update your organisation address
3. Tax will be recalculated for future invoices

## Annual Billing Shows as Monthly

When you sign up for annual billing, you pay for a full year upfront. The invoice shows the total annual amount, not a monthly breakdown.

If you see it listed as "monthly" in your payment records but you paid annually, this is correct — annual billing appears as one yearly charge.

## Requesting a Refund

DonorDesk's refund policy:
- Monthly plans: No refund for partial months
- Annual plans: Pro-rated refund for unused months (contact support)

To request a refund:
- Contact **support@donordesk.online** with your details and reason
- Refunds are reviewed on a case-by-case basis
