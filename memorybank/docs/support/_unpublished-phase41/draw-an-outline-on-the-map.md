# Mark a place or draw its outline on the map

Open **Places**, choose a place and pick **Place on map**.

- **Drop a pin**: click the spot, use **My location**, or type coordinates. Arrow buttons nudge the pin. The map suggests the district or ward it falls in; you decide.
- **Draw an outline**: choose **Draw area** (or **Draw route** for a road), click each corner and finish at the first point. DonorDesk refuses an outline that crosses itself or has no area and says why.
- Your typed location text is never replaced. Remove a pin or outline any time with **Clear**.
- Everything on the map is also in the table beside it, and works with the keyboard.
