# Use your own boundaries

Administrators: **Settings → Map boundaries**. Choose a country and level (districts, sub-districts, wards), then upload a GeoJSON file. Each unit needs a name and, ideally, the official P-code in the same field as your Places list. Units that match a place join by code first, then by name.

Your boundaries are shown to your organisation only and replace the built-in ones for that country and level. Remove the file to go back. Sources and licences of the built-in boundaries are listed on **Map sources and licences**.
