# Reference: Locked Figures and Reopening a Report

Once a report is approved, submitted or closed, the indicator figures for its period are locked so that the approved numbers do not change under it.

## What you will see

- On the report's **Inputs → Indicators** page, a notice: *"This report's figures are locked."* The fields are read-only.
- If you try to save a value anyway, the message says what to do: *reopen the report to correct them*.

## Which reports are locked

| Report status | Figures |
|---|---|
| Not started, in progress, drafted, under review | Open for editing |
| Approved, submitted, closed | Locked |

## How to correct a locked figure

1. Open the report and open the report menu and choose **Reopen for changes**.
2. Say why you are reopening it. The reason is kept in the audit trail, with who reopened it and the approval it replaces.
3. Correct the figure, then approve the report again.

Reopening returns the report to draft, so it must be approved again.
