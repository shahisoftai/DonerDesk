# User Roles and Permissions

Every person in a workspace has one **role**. The role decides what they can see and do. DonorDesk has seven roles.

| Role | Typical person | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| **Admin** | Head of programmes, operations lead | Everything, including team, billing, settings and audit log. |
| **Project Manager** | Programme manager | Runs projects: logframe, templates, evidence, reports, approval and export. |
| **M&E Officer** | Monitoring and evaluation | Logframe, indicators (enter and verify), evidence, activities, drafting and editing reports. |
| **Grants Officer** | Donor reporting / grants | Templates, evidence upload, generating, editing and exporting reports, confidentiality overrides. |
| **Field Officer** | Field staff | Log activities and upload evidence. |
| **Compliance Officer** | Compliance / risk | Verify evidence, manage the compliance checklist, view the audit log. |
| **Viewer** | Board, donor liaison, auditor | Read-only: browse projects and export reports. |

## Project assignments

On each project's **Team** tab you assign workspace members to that project with a **project role**. Access to a project's data follows these assignments as well as the workspace role.

See [How to invite team members](/support/how-to/invite-team-members) and [How to change a member's role](/support/how-to/manage-team-roles-permissions).
