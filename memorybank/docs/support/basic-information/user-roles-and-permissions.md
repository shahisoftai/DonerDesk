# User Roles and Permissions in DonorDesk

Every person on your DonorDesk workspace has a role. The role determines what they can see and do.

## Default Roles

### Owner

The owner is the person who created the organisation. They have full access to everything, including:
- Managing billing and subscriptions
- Deleting the workspace
- Managing all projects and team members
- Changing organisation settings

There is only one owner per organisation.

### Admin

Admins manage the day-to-day operations:
- Create and manage projects
- Invite and manage team members
- Edit organisation settings
- View all reports and evidence
- Cannot manage billing

### Project Manager

Project managers oversee specific projects:
- Create and edit project details
- Manage project team members
- Upload evidence and log activities
- Generate and edit reports
- Cannot delete the organisation

### M&E Officer

M&E (Monitoring and Evaluation) Officers focus on data quality:
- Update indicator values
- Upload and tag evidence
- Log activities
- View and edit compliance checklists
- Cannot manage team or billing

### Field Officer

Field officers capture field-level data:
- Log activities
- Upload evidence
- View the projects they are assigned to
- Cannot manage projects or team

### Grants Officer

Grants officers manage donor reporting:
- Create and submit reports
- Manage compliance checklists
- Review evidence
- Coordinate with project managers
- Cannot manage team or billing

### Compliance Officer

Compliance officers ensure quality and standards:
- Review and approve reports
- Verify evidence
- Manage compliance checklists
- View audit logs
- Cannot manage billing or team

## How Roles Are Assigned

When you invite a team member, you choose their role. You can change a role at any time from the Team page.

## Project-Level Roles

In addition to organisation-wide roles, users can be assigned specific roles within a project:
- Project Manager
- M&E Focal Person
- Grants/Reporting Focal Person

These project-level roles help distribute responsibility across large teams.
