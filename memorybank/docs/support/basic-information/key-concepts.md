# Key Concepts in DonorDesk

This guide explains the main terms and concepts used in DonorDesk.

## Workspace

A workspace is your organisation's space in DonorDesk. It contains all your projects, team members, templates, and reports. Each workspace is completely separated from others — no organisation can see another organisation's data.

## Organisation

Your organisation profile stores your NGO or programme's details: name, type, country, sectors, and contact information. This appears on reports and helps your team identify which organisation they belong to.

## Project

A project is a single donor-funded initiative within your organisation. For example:
- "Nutrition Programme — Rohingya Refugees 2026"
- "Maternal Health — District X"

Each project has its own:
- Logframe and indicators
- Evidence library
- Activity log
- Reporting periods
- Team

## Logframe

A logframe is a hierarchical plan that shows how your project works. It has four levels:

| Level | What it means | Example |
|-------|--------------|---------|
| **Goal** | The overall aim | "Reduce maternal mortality in District X" |
| **Outcome** | The change you expect | "400 pregnant women access antenatal care" |
| **Output** | What you will produce | "20 health workers trained in ANC" |
| **Activity** | The tasks you will do | "Conduct 10 training sessions" |

## Indicator

An indicator measures progress at each logframe level. Each indicator has:
- A **target** (what you aim to achieve)
- A **baseline** (where you start)
- A **current value** (what you have achieved so far)
- A **unit** (e.g., number, percentage, yes/no)

Example indicator: "Number of pregnant women receiving 4+ ANC visits — Target: 400, Baseline: 0, Unit: women"

## Evidence

Evidence is any document, photo, or file that proves your work happened. DonorDesk supports:
- Photos
- Attendance sheets
- Training records
- Field visit reports
- Procurement documents
- Monitoring reports
- Any other supporting file (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, JPG, PNG)

Each evidence item can be tagged, verified, and linked to specific activities or indicators.

## Reporting Period

A reporting period is a time window for which you prepare a donor report. Common examples:
- Quarterly (every 3 months)
- Semi-annual (twice a year)
- Annual (once a year)

Each reporting period has a deadline and a status (Not Started, In Progress, Submitted, etc.).

## Compliance Checklist

The compliance checklist automatically identifies gaps in your evidence and documentation. It checks:
- Missing evidence for activities
- Unverified indicators
- Unsupported claims in your report
- Missing annexes required by donors

Each item has a severity level (Low, Medium, High, Critical).

## Report Draft

A report draft is the narrative report you submit to your donor. It is built section by section. Each section can be:
- Written manually
- Generated with AI assistance
- Edited and approved

## AI Report Draft

DonorDesk can generate an AI-assisted draft of your report using the evidence, activities, and indicator data you have already entered. The AI produces text that you can review, edit, and approve before export.

## Readability Score (Report Readiness)

The readiness score tells you how complete your report is before submission. It combines:
- Section completion (25%)
- Indicator updates (20%)
- Evidence completeness (25%)
- Compliance checklist (20%)
- Approval status (10%)

## Storage

DonorDesk stores your files using one of two approaches:
- **Google Drive (link-first)** — Files stay in your own Google Drive; DonorDesk stores only a link
- **Local storage / R2** — Files are stored on DonorDesk's servers

## Users and Roles

Users in DonorDesk have different roles that control what they can see and do:

| Role | What they can do |
|------|----------------|
| Owner | Full access including billing |
| Admin | Manage team, projects, settings |
| Project Manager | Manage projects and reports |
| M&E Officer | Update indicators and evidence |
| Field Officer | Log activities |
| Grants Officer | Manage reports and compliance |
| Compliance Officer | Review and approve |

## Plan / Tier

Your plan determines how much you can use DonorDesk:
- **Starter (Free)** — 1 project, 1 user, 1 GB storage, 5 AI drafts/month
- **Team ($59/month)** — 5 projects, 5 users, 25 GB storage, 100 AI drafts/month
- **Growth ($149/month)** — 20 projects, 15 users, 100 GB storage, 500 AI drafts/month
- **Enterprise (Custom)** — Unlimited, with SLA and dedicated support

Annual billing gives 2 months free.
