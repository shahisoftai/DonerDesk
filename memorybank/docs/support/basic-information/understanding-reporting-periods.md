# Understanding Reporting Periods in DonorDesk

A reporting period is a defined time window for which you prepare and submit a donor report. It is one of the most important concepts in DonorDesk because all your evidence, activities, and indicator updates are organised around reporting periods.

## What is a Reporting Period?

Think of a reporting period as a "bucket" that collects everything that happens in a specific time frame. When a donor asks for a "Quarterly Progress Report for Q3 2026", that is a reporting period.

The reporting period contains:
- Activities logged during that time
- Evidence uploaded during that time
- Indicator values updated for that time
- A draft of your donor report
- A compliance checklist for that period

## Common Reporting Frequencies

| Frequency | What it means | Example |
|-----------|---------------|---------|
| **Monthly** | Report every month | 12 reports per year |
| **Quarterly** | Report every 3 months | 4 reports per year |
| **Semi-annual** | Report twice a year | 2 reports per year |
| **Annual** | Report once a year | 1 report per year |
| **Final report** | One report at project end | 1 report total |

Your reporting frequency is set when you create a project and can be changed later.

## Reporting Period Statuses

Each reporting period has a status that tracks its lifecycle:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Not Started** | The period has begun but no work has been done |
| **In Progress** | Work on the report has started |
| **Evidence Collection** | Gathering and uploading evidence for this period |
| **Draft Generated** | AI or manual draft has been created |
| **Under Review** | Team is reviewing the report |
| **Approved** | Report has been approved internally |
| **Submitted** | Report has been sent to the donor |
| **Closed** | Reporting period is complete |

## Creating a Reporting Period

1. Go to your project → **Reports** tab
2. Click **New Reporting Period**
3. Fill in:
   - **Report type** — e.g., "Quarterly Progress Report"
   - **Start date** — First day of the reporting period
   - **End date** — Last day of the reporting period
   - **Report deadline** — When the report is due to the donor
   - **Internal review deadline** — When internal review should be complete
   - **Donor template** — Link a donor template if you have one
   - **Responsible officer** — Who is in charge of this report
4. Click **Create**

## The Reporting Period Dashboard

Each reporting period has its own dashboard showing:

- **Readiness score** — How complete is this report (0-100%)
- **Sections** — Status of each report section (Not Started, Drafted, Needs Review, Approved)
- **Indicator updates** — Have all indicators been updated for this period?
- **Evidence completeness** — How much evidence is linked vs. missing
- **Missing evidence items** — What the compliance checklist has identified
- **Open comments** — Any unresolved review comments
- **Draft report** — Your current draft version
- **Export options** — Download as PDF, Word, Excel, or ZIP

## Indicator Updates Per Period

Indicator values are recorded **for each reporting period**, not just once. This is because donors want to see progress over time.

For example:
- Indicator: "Number of women receiving ANC checkups"
- Baseline (start of project): 0
- Q1 value: 45 women
- Q2 value: 120 women (cumulative)
- Q3 value: 285 women (cumulative)

Each value is linked to a specific reporting period.

### How to Update Indicators

1. Open your reporting period
2. Click **Indicators** tab
3. You will see a spreadsheet grid with all your logframe indicators
4. Enter values for the current period
5. Add comments and data sources
6. Click **Submit** to verify the row (verified rows are locked)

### Importing from Google Sheets

If you track indicator data in Google Sheets:

1. Click **Import from Google Sheets**
2. Authorise access to your Google Drive
3. Select the spreadsheet with your indicator data
4. Map indicator codes to columns
5. Preview the data
6. Click **Apply to Grid**

## Compliance Checklist Per Period

When you create a reporting period, a compliance checklist is automatically generated. It checks:

- Which activities have evidence
- Which evidence is verified
- Whether report claims are supported
- Whether required annexes are attached

Items appear with severity levels (Low, Medium, High, Critical) and can be assigned to team members.

## The Report Draft

The report draft is the narrative document you will submit to your donor. It is organised by the sections in your donor template.

Each section can be:
- Written manually
- Generated by AI
- Edited after generation

DonorDesk generates an AI draft using your:
- Evidence content
- Activity narratives
- Indicator values and trends
- Previous period comparisons
- Compliance checklist status

## Timeline View

You can see all your reporting periods on a timeline:

1. Go to your project → **Reports** tab
2. Click **Timeline View**

This shows:
- Past and future reporting periods
- Deadline dates
- Current status of each period
- Days remaining until each deadline
