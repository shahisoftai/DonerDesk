# Getting Started with DonorDesk

Welcome to DonorDesk! This guide walks you through your first steps.

## Step 1: Create Your Account

1. Go to **https://donordesk.online**
2. Click **Get started** or **Sign up**
3. Enter your:
   - Full name
   - Work email address
   - Password
   - Organisation name
   - Organisation type (e.g., Local NGO, INGO, Government programme)
   - Country
   - Primary sector (e.g., Health, Nutrition, WASH, Education)
4. Click **Create account**

You will receive an email to verify your account. Click the link in the email to activate it.

## Step 2: Complete the Setup Wizard

After logging in for the first time, DonorDesk guides you through a setup wizard:

1. **Connect Google Drive** (optional) — Link your Google Drive to use it as file storage
2. **Organisation Profile** — Confirm your organisation details
3. **Default Reporting Profile** — Set your default reporting preferences
4. **Invite Your Team** — Add colleagues so they can collaborate (optional, can skip)
5. **Accept Terms** — Read and accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy

Complete as many steps as you like. You can finish the rest later from your dashboard.

## Step 3: Create Your First Project

After the setup wizard, click **Create your first project** or go to **Projects → New Project**.

Fill in the project details:
- **Project title** — A clear name for the project
- **Project code** — A short code (e.g., NUT-2026)
- **Donor name** — The funder (e.g., UNHCR, EU, USAID)
- **Implementing organisation** — Your organisation
- **Partner organisation** — Any partner (optional)
- **Country** and **Region/District**
- **Sector** — The main sector (e.g., Nutrition, Health)
- **Start date** and **End date**
- **Reporting frequency** — How often you report (Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-annual, Annual)
- **Project description** — A brief description

Click **Create Project**.

## Step 4: Upload Your Donor Template (Optional)

A donor template is the report format your donor requires. If you have one:

1. Go to your project → **Templates** tab
2. Click **Upload Template**
3. Select your file (PDF, DOCX, or DOC)
4. DonorDesk will extract the sections and requirements automatically

You can also create sections manually.

## Step 5: Build Your Logframe

1. Go to your project → **Logframe** tab
2. Click **Add item**
3. Choose the level: Goal, Outcome, Output, or Activity
4. Fill in the details: code, title, description

For indicators:
1. Go to the **Logframe** tab
2. Click **Add Indicator** next to the relevant item
3. Fill in: name, type (Number, Percentage, Yes/No, etc.), baseline, target, unit

## Step 6: Create a Reporting Period

1. Go to your project → **Reports** tab
2. Click **New Reporting Period**
3. Select the **report type** (e.g., Quarterly Progress Report)
4. Set the **start date**, **end date**, and **donor deadline**
5. Link your donor template if you have one
6. Assign a **responsible reporting officer**

Click **Create Period**.

## Step 7: Upload Evidence and Log Activities

As your project runs:

**Log an activity:**
1. Go to your project → **Activities** tab
2. Click **Log Activity**
3. Fill in: title, description, date, location, participants
4. Link to relevant outputs and indicators
5. Click **Save Activity**

**Upload evidence:**
1. Go to your project → **Evidence** tab
2. Click **Upload Evidence**
3. Select your file (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, JPG, PNG, TXT)
4. Fill in metadata: title, evidence type, related activity, date, location
5. Click **Upload**

AI will automatically suggest tags for your evidence based on its content.

## Step 8: Generate an AI Report Draft

1. Go to your project → **Reports** tab
2. Open your reporting period
3. Click **Generate Report Draft**

DonorDesk uses your uploaded evidence, activities, and indicator data to generate report sections. Review each section, make edits, and approve.

## Step 9: Export Your Report

1. Go to your reporting period → **Export**
2. Choose your format: PDF, Word (DOCX), Excel, or Evidence Pack (ZIP)
3. Review any pre-export warnings
4. Click **Download**

## Quick Tips

- **Use Google Drive** — Link your Drive to save storage space
- **Update indicators regularly** — Keep values current so AI drafts are accurate
- **Tag evidence well** — Good tags help link evidence to report sections
- **Check the readiness score** — It tells you what is missing before you export
- **Invite your team early** — Collaboration makes reporting faster

## Need Help?

If you get stuck at any step, contact support at **support@donordesk.online** or visit the support portal.
