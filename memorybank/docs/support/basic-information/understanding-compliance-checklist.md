# Understanding the Compliance Checklist

The compliance checklist is a live list of what is missing or weak before a report goes to the donor.

## What it checks

- Missing evidence and incomplete evidence details
- Unverified indicators
- Unsupported report claims
- Missing annexes, procurement documents or approvals
- Missing disaggregation
- Late activity updates
- Sensitive data warnings
- Unreviewed AI output
- Donor requirements from the template

## Severity and status

Severity: **Low, Medium, High, Critical**. Status: **Open, In progress, Resolved, Accepted risk, Not applicable**. Resolving needs a note; accepting a high-severity risk needs authority.

## Automatic

The list is generated from your template, logframe, activities and evidence and refreshes as data changes. Coverage gaps in a report create items without duplicating or overwriting your earlier decisions.

## Where you see it

- **Compliance** in the main menu (all projects) and each project's compliance page
- **Report checks** in the report editor
- The export gate

Blocking items must be cleared, or accepted with a reason, before approval and export.
