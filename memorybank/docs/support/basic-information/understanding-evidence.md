# Understanding Evidence

Evidence is the file-based proof behind your numbers and claims: attendance sheets, photos, distribution lists, training records, monitoring and field visit reports, Kobo/ODK exports, procurement and approval documents, beneficiary lists, meeting minutes, case studies, financial documents, donor communication and more.

## What you record

Title, type, reporting period, confidentiality, location, and links to activities and indicators.

## Where files live

- **Your own Google Drive** (link-first): files stay in your Drive, DonorDesk keeps a reference. No quota used.
- **DonorDesk storage**: managed uploads that count toward your plan's storage (Starter 1 GB, Team 25 GB, Growth 100 GB).

## Life of an evidence file

1. **Upload** or link from Drive; text is read from documents.
2. **AI tag suggestions** (type, related activity/indicator, period, summary, sensitivity warnings). You accept, edit or reject.
3. **Link** it to activities and indicators.
4. **Verify** it (Admin, Project Manager, M&E Officer or Compliance Officer).
5. It is **cited** in reports and counted by the compliance checklist.

Statuses: Uploaded, AI tagged, Pending review, Verified, Needs correction, Rejected, Archived, Unverified. See [Evidence verification](/support/getting-started/evidence-verification).

## Confidentiality

Public, Internal, Sensitive and Highly sensitive. Sensitive files are withheld from AI drafting, and exports that cite them need an Admin or Grants Officer to confirm.

## What makes good evidence

- Dated, legible, and matching the reporting period.
- Linked to the right indicator or activity.
- Verified.
