# Reference: AI Usage and Estimated Cost

**Settings → AI usage** lists the last sections the AI writer worked on, and what each one cost.

## What the figures mean

- **Est. cost** is the estimated amount the AI provider charges for that section, in US dollars. It is calculated from the length of the request and the reply, at the provider's list price.
- **Estimated provider cost this month** adds up the cost from the first day of the calendar month (UTC).
- These are **estimates**. Your provider's invoice is the figure to rely on.

Your plan limits how many AI drafts you can make. The cost figure is separate: it shows what the provider charges, not what your plan allows.

## Spend alerts

If your estimated AI spend for a month passes the threshold set by DonorDesk, an alert is written for the operator. Contact support if you want a different threshold.

## Why a figure may show a dash

A dash (—) means no cost was recorded for that section, for example because it was written without the AI.
