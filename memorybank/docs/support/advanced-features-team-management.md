# Advanced: Team Management Best Practices

This guide helps organisation administrators manage their DonorDesk workspace effectively and securely.

## Principle of Least Privilege

Give team members only the permissions they need to do their job:

| Role | When to use |
|------|------------|
| **Owner** | Only the primary account holder — 1 person |
| **Admin** | Senior managers who need full access |
| **Project Manager** | Programme leads who manage specific projects |
| **Grants Officer** | Staff responsible for donor reporting |
| **Compliance Officer** | Quality assurance staff |
| **M&E Officer** | M&E staff who enter data |
| **Field Officer** | Field staff who log activities — minimum permissions |

## Project-Based Access

Instead of giving everyone organisation-wide access:

1. Invite people to specific projects only
2. Use project-level roles (M&E Focal, Reporting Focal)
3. Regularly review who has access to what

This reduces risk and keeps the workspace organised.

## Onboarding New Team Members

When a new person joins:

1. **Create their account** (invite via email)
2. **Assign the minimum role needed** — do not over-permission
3. **Give project-specific access** — only the projects they work on
4. **Brief them on DonorDesk** — point them to the getting started guide
5. **Assign a buddy** — pair new staff with experienced users

## Offboarding Team Members

When someone leaves:

1. Go to **Team** settings
2. Find their name
3. Click **Remove from workspace**
4. **Reassign their work** — check what they own and reassign:
   - Their projects
   - Pending compliance items
   - Active report drafts
5. **Review their audit log** — check for any concerns

## Role Changes

When someone's responsibilities change:

1. Go to **Team**
2. Click on the person
3. Change their role
4. Update their project access if needed
5. Notify them of the change

## Two-Factor Authentication (Coming Soon)

When two-factor authentication (2FA) is enabled on your workspace:
- All team members will be required to set up 2FA
- This protects against unauthorized access even if a password is compromised

To enforce 2FA:
1. Go to **Settings → Security**
2. Click **Require Two-Factor Authentication**
3. Set a deadline for team members to comply
4. Team members will receive an email with instructions

## Activity Monitoring

You can monitor workspace activity through the audit log:

1. Go to **Settings → Audit Log**
2. Filter by:
   - Recent actions by specific users
   - Changes to specific projects
   - Admin-level actions
3. Export logs for your records

## Setting Up Delegation

If you need someone to cover while you are away:

1. Assign a colleague as a temporary Admin
2. Set their role to Admin temporarily
3. Change it back when you return

## Team Size Recommendations by Plan

| Plan | Recommended team size |
|------|---------------------|
| Starter | 1 (just you) |
| Team | 3-5 (programme lead, M&E, 1-2 field officers, grants) |
| Growth | 5-15 (add compliance officer, additional PMs) |
| Enterprise | Unlimited — structure as needed |

## Useful Team Structures

### Small NGO (Starter/Team plan)

- Owner/Admin: Programme Director
- M&E Officer: 1 person
- Field Officers: 2-3 people
- Grants Officer: often the director or a shared role

### Larger NGO (Growth/Enterprise plan)

- Admin: Operations Manager
- Project Managers: 2-3 (one per major project)
- M&E Officers: 2 (one senior, one junior)
- Compliance Officer: 1
- Grants Officers: 1-2
- Field Officers: As many as needed

## Regular Access Reviews

Every 3-6 months, review who has access:

1. Go to **Team** settings
2. Export the team list
3. Remove anyone who has left
4. Change roles for anyone whose responsibilities have changed
5. Verify that project access is still appropriate
