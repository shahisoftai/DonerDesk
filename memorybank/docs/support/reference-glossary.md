# Glossary of Terms

This glossary explains the key terms used in DonorDesk.

## A

**Activity**
A specific task or event carried out as part of a project. Examples: training sessions, distribution events, field visits.

**AI Draft**
A report section or full report generated automatically by DonorDesk's AI, based on your project data.

**API (Application Programming Interface)**
A way for developers to connect DonorDesk to other software systems.

**Approval Workflow**
The process of submitting, reviewing, and approving a report before it goes to the donor.

## B

**Baseline**
The starting value of an indicator at the beginning of a project, before any activities have been carried out.

## C

**Compliance Checklist**
A list of items that DonorDesk automatically generates to identify missing evidence or documentation in your project.

**Cumulative Achievement**
The total value of an indicator from the project start date to the current date (not just the reporting period).

## D

**Dashboard**
The main page you see after logging in, showing an overview of your projects, upcoming deadlines, and key metrics.

## E

**Evidence**
Documents, photos, or files that prove your project activities happened and achieved results. Examples: attendance sheets, photos, training reports.

**Evidence Library**
The centralised storage for all evidence files in a project.

**Export**
Downloading your report in a specific format (PDF, Word, Excel, or ZIP) for submission to your donor.

## F

**Field Officer**
A user role for staff who work in the field and log activities and upload evidence.

## G

**Goal**
The overall aim of a project — the big-picture change the project aims to achieve.

## I

**Indicator**
A measurement that tracks progress toward a logframe objective. Each indicator has a name, target, and unit.

**Indicator Update**
Recording the value of an indicator for a specific reporting period.

## L

**Logframe (Logical Framework)**
A hierarchical planning tool showing a project's Goal, Outcomes, Outputs, Activities, and Indicators.

## M

**M&E (Monitoring and Evaluation)**
The process of tracking progress and measuring results. M&E Officers in DonorDesk manage this process.

## O

**Outcome**
A change that happens as a result of your project's outputs. Written in past tense (e.g., "400 women accessed antenatal care").

**Output**
A concrete product or service produced by your project (e.g., "20 health workers trained").

## P

**Period Achievement**
The value of an indicator achieved within a specific reporting period (not cumulative).

**Project**
A single donor-funded initiative within your organisation. Each project has its own logframe, evidence, activities, and reports.

**Project Manager**
A user role for the person responsible for managing a specific project.

## R

**Readiness Score**
A percentage (0-100%) showing how complete a reporting period is. Based on sections, indicators, evidence, compliance, and approvals.

**Report Draft**
The draft narrative document that will be submitted to the donor. Consists of sections that can be written manually or generated with AI.

**Reporting Period**
A defined time window (monthly, quarterly, etc.) for which you prepare a donor report.

**RLS (Row-Level Security)**
A database security technique that ensures each organisation's data is completely isolated from others.

## S

**Source Reference**
A citation in an AI-generated report that links a statement back to the original evidence, activity, or indicator data it came from.

## T

**Target**
The value an indicator aims to achieve by the end of the project.

## U

**Upload**
Transferring a file from your device to DonorDesk's evidence library.

## V

**Verification**
The process of a reviewer confirming that evidence or indicator data is accurate and valid.

## W

**Workspace**
Your organisation's private space in DonorDesk. Contains all your projects, team members, and settings. Completely isolated from other organisations' workspaces.
