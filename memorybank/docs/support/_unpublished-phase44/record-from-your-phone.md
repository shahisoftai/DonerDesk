# Record from your phone

Tap **Record** in the middle of the bottom bar and choose what you want to record: an activity, a number, a file or an expense. Fill the few questions and tap Save. No signal? Keep going: it is saved on your phone and sent when you are back online. Open **Waiting** to see what has not been sent yet; you can try again or remove an entry there. Before leaving for the field, open **More → Ready for offline** and download your project.

If you planned an activity, tap **Finished?** on it in Today and say what happened. Photos are made smaller before they are sent. (Unpublished draft: publish only after a person has tried it on a phone.)
