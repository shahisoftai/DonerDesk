# How to Recover Lost or Changed Data

Act quickly and stop editing the affected item. Most things can be recovered from history inside DonorDesk.

## A report section was changed or deleted

1. Open the report editor and select the section.
2. In the inspector open **History**. Every save is a version (written by the AI, edited, regenerated, rewritten with AI, number corrected from evidence).
3. Pick the earlier version and restore it.
4. For the whole report, use **⋯ → Version history** to see earlier drafts. Older drafts are kept when a new draft supersedes them.

If you regenerated a section and the writer failed, your previous text was kept automatically.

## Indicator values were changed

Open the indicator page and check **History** for each update: value, who entered it, verification decisions. Re-enter the earlier value in the grid and save. Earlier Excel exports of the indicator table also help.

## Evidence was deleted

Only Admins can delete evidence.

1. Check **Settings → Audit log** to see who deleted it and when.
2. If the file was linked from **Google Drive**, the original is still in your Drive (check the Drive trash too). Upload or link it again and re-link it to the activity or indicator.
3. Contact support@donordesk.online quickly if the file was in DonorDesk-managed storage; recovery is not guaranteed.

## An activity or checklist decision was wrong

- Activities can be re-submitted or revised. Reviewers can send them back with a note.
- Checklist decisions (resolved, accepted risk, not applicable) can be changed by someone with checklist permission; the note history is kept.

## A project was archived by mistake

Open the project's **Settings** and click **Restore project** (you need a free active-project slot on your plan).

## A team member's role or access changed

Check the audit log for **role changed** events and set the role back on the Team page.

## Prevention

- Export approved reports and indicator tables regularly.
- Keep original evidence in your own Google Drive.
- Do not delete evidence that is linked to reports.
- Approve sections only when finished: approved sections are locked for that version.

If you cannot recover something, email support@donordesk.online with the project, item and time it was last correct.
