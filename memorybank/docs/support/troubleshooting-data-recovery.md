# How to Recover Lost or Deleted Data

This guide explains what to do if you accidentally lose or delete data in DonorDesk.

## Important: Act Quickly

The sooner you act, the better the chance of recovery. Contact support immediately if you have lost data.

## Types of Data Loss

### Accidentally Deleted Evidence

**What happened:** You deleted an evidence file that was needed.

**Recovery options:**

1. **Check the audit log** — See who deleted it and when
2. **Check your Google Drive** — If the file was linked from Drive, the original may still exist
3. **Contact support** — We may be able to restore from backup

**Prevention:** Before deleting evidence, verify it is not linked to active compliance items.

### Deleted Report Section

**What happened:** Content in a report section was accidentally deleted.

**Recovery options:**

1. **Check the autosave** — DonorDesk autosaves periodically
2. **Check version history** — Earlier versions may exist
3. **Regenerate with AI** — If the underlying data is intact

**Prevention:** Export important reports regularly.

### Indicator Values Reset

**What happened:** Indicator values were accidentally changed or deleted.

**Recovery options:**

1. **Check the indicator history** — If update history is available
2. **Check exports** — Earlier Excel exports may have the data
3. **Re-enter from source documents** — Use your field records

### Deleted Activity

**What happened:** An activity log entry was deleted.

**Recovery options:**

1. **Check the audit log** — Shows who deleted it and when
2. **Check linked evidence** — Evidence files may still exist
3. **Re-log the activity** — Use your field notes to recreate

## Data Backup

### What Is Backed Up

DonorDesk maintains backups of:
- All database records
- File metadata
- Audit logs

### What Is Not Backed Up

Backups do not include:
- Files stored only in Google Drive (these rely on Drive's backup)
- Export files you have downloaded

### Backup Frequency

Backups are created:
- Daily incremental backups
- Weekly full backups
- Retained for 30 days

## Contacting Support for Recovery

### What to Include

When contacting **support@donordesk.online** for data recovery:

1. Your registered email
2. Organisation name
3. Description of what was lost
4. Approximate date/time of loss
5. What you have already tried

### What to Expect

- We will acknowledge within 24 hours
- Recovery assessment may take 1-2 business days
- Not all data can be recovered
- We will tell you what was and was not recoverable

## Prevention Best Practices

### Regular Exports

1. Export indicator data monthly
2. Export completed reports
3. Download important evidence

### Permission Controls

1. Limit who can delete evidence
2. Require confirmation for deletions
3. Review delete permissions regularly

### Audit Log Monitoring

1. Check audit log periodically
2. Look for unexpected deletions
3. Address issues quickly

## What Cannot Be Recovered

In some cases, data cannot be recovered:
- Files deleted from Google Drive (for linked files)
- Data deleted more than 30 days ago
- Data lost due to account deletion
- Data from periods before certain feature implementations
