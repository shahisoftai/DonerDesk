# Understanding the Logframe Hierarchy

The logframe is a hierarchy that shows how your project is structured. Understanding this hierarchy is key to using DonorDesk effectively.

## The Four Levels

A standard logframe has four levels:

```
Goal
  └── Outcome(s)
        └── Output(s)
              └── Activity/Actions
```

### Goal (Top Level)

The Goal is the ultimate aim of your project — the big-picture change you want to see.

**Example Goal:**
"Reduce maternal and neonatal mortality in District Kubwa by 30% by December 2027"

**Characteristics:**
- One Goal per project (usually)
- Written by senior management and donors
- Rarely changes during the project
- Not directly measurable — outcomes contribute to it

### Outcome(s)

Outcomes are the changes that happen as a result of your project's outputs. They describe what beneficiaries do or experience differently.

**Example Outcome:**
"400 pregnant women in target communities accessed quality antenatal care services"

**Characteristics:**
- Usually 2-5 outcomes per project
- Written in past tense (the change that occurred)
- Directly linked to the Goal
- Measured by indicators

### Output(s)

Outputs are the concrete products or services your project delivers.

**Example Output:**
"20 community health workers trained in Antenatal Care screening protocols"

**Characteristics:**
- More numerous than outcomes
- Under your project's direct control
- Produced within the project timeframe
- Measured by indicators

### Activity/Actions

Activities are the day-to-day tasks your team carries out.

**Example Activity:**
"Conduct 10-day training programme for 20 CHWs on ANC screening"

**Characteristics:**
- The most detailed level
- Things your team actually does
- Can be directly managed
- Usually numerous

## The Logic Chain

The hierarchy works because:

```
Activities → produce → Outputs → contribute to → Outcomes → achieve → Goal
```

If activities are completed as planned, they produce outputs. Outputs contribute to outcomes. Outcomes together achieve the goal.

## Indicators at Each Level

Each level can have indicators to measure progress:

| Level | Indicator example | What it measures |
|-------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Goal | Maternal mortality rate | Long-term change |
| Outcome | ANC visit rate | Service utilisation |
| Output | Number trained | Deliverables |
| Activity | Sessions conducted | Work done |

## Adding Items in DonorDesk

### Adding a Goal

1. Go to the **Logframe** tab
2. Click **Add Item**
3. Select **Goal** as the level
4. Enter code and title
5. Save

### Adding Child Items

1. Click the **+** icon next to an existing item
2. Select the child level (Outcome under Goal, Output under Outcome, Activity under Output)
3. Enter details

The hierarchy builds automatically.

## Reading the Logframe Tree

In DonorDesk, the logframe shows as an expandable tree:

```
▼ Goal: Reduce maternal mortality
    ▼ Outcome: 400 women access ANC
        ▼ Output: 20 CHWs trained
            • Activity: Training session 1
            • Activity: Training session 2
        ► Output: Equipment supplied
    ► Outcome: ...
```

Click arrows to expand/collapse levels.

## Common Mistakes

### Confusing Levels

**Wrong:** Putting activities at the Outcome level

**Right:** Activities go at the Activity level, outcomes describe the changes they produce

### Too Many Levels

Stick to the four levels. If you need more detail, use sub-indicators.

### Vague Wording

**Wrong:** "Improve capacity"

**Right:** "50 community health workers demonstrate improved ANC screening skills"

## Why the Hierarchy Matters

### For Compliance

Donors want to see:
- Activities were implemented (outputs produced)
- Outputs contributed to outcomes
- Outcomes moved toward the goal

### For Reporting

AI-generated reports use the hierarchy to:
- Write coherent narratives
- Link evidence to the right level
- Show progress logically

### For Management

The hierarchy helps you:
- Track progress systematically
- Identify where things are falling behind
- Make informed decisions
