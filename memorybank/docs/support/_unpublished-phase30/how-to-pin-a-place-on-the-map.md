# Pin a place on the map

Most of the time, typing a town in an activity's **Location** field is enough — the map knows thousands of places and suggests them as you type. When the work happens somewhere the map does not know — a new camp, a village cluster, a spot outside town — you can pin it by hand instead.

## Pin the place

1. Open the project and choose **Activities → Add planned activity**.
2. Fill in the title and dates as usual.
3. Open **More details** and next to the Location field choose **Place it on the map**.
4. Click the map where the work happens. A red pin drops there, and the coordinates appear under the map.

You can drag the map to move around and zoom in for a closer spot, then click again to move the pin. **Remove the pin** takes it back off.

5. Save the activity as usual. The pin saves with it, and the activity appears on the map exactly where you put it.

## Good to know

- **The Location text still matters.** If you type a name as well, the map shows the activity under that name at your pinned spot. With no text, it shows as *Pinned place*.
- **Suggestions never overwrite what you type.** The list under the Location field only offers places the map knows; ignoring them is fine — that is what the pin is for.
- **A pin is part of the activity**, like the title or the dates: it appears for everyone who can see the plan, and it moves only when someone edits the activity.
- If the coordinates are ever wrong, open the same form flow on the activity (or ask a colleague with access) and click the map again to re-pin, or remove the pin and use a place name instead.
