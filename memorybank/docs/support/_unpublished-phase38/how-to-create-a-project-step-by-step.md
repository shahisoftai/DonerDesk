# How to Create a Project, Step by Step

Draft only: this article is registered (in the support file map and categories) when the wizard has been checked in a signed-in browser with a real account, as the other unpublished drafts are.

The guided wizard sets up a whole project in ten steps, with or without project documents. When you start the project, it is ready to use: nothing has to be finished on other pages first, except the few things listed at the end.

## Before you begin

Only an administrator can create a project. You can stop at any time: your answers are saved as you leave each box, and you can come back to the same draft from **New project**.

## With documents or without

On the first step you can add up to five documents (a proposal, a logframe, a work plan, a budget). They are read for you, and everything found is shown for you to confirm. Reading documents needs the AI service and Google Drive to be switched on for your organisation; if either is off, you are told what is missing and you can answer the steps yourself. Nothing is created until you start the project.

## The ten steps

1. **Start.** Say who the donor is, the sector and the country. The sector's starting answers (how often to report, the usual report layouts and due dates, how values are checked, when entry opens and closes, usual risks and a starting dashboard) fill only what is empty. They are never chosen by donor: what a donor requires comes from your own documents and report layouts.
2. **Project and team.** Name, code, dates, currency, a short summary, and the people who will run the project. A person without an account can be named now and invited when the project starts or later.
3. **Results framework.** The goal, outcomes, outputs and activities, each under the right parent.
4. **Indicators.** For each: what is measured, the result it belongs to, the kind of number, starting value, target, how often, who is responsible and which way is better.
5. **Places.** Where the project works, and its project area.
6. **Work plan.** Each piece of work, its dates, the project activity it belongs to, its place, who leads it and which indicators it feeds. Links are suggested with the reason for each; nothing is linked until you accept it.
7. **Data entry.** How each indicator's numbers get in (a form, another tool, typed in, or worked out from others), how values are checked, and when entry opens and closes for each period.
8. **Reporting.** How often, which kinds of report, their layout and due dates, and the calendar that follows from the project's dates.
9. **Budget and finance.** Funding, budget lines placed on the activities they pay for, and the currency you report in. Funding and budget are shown side by side; a difference is stated and needs your acceptance. If your project does not track money here, say so once.
10. **Review and launch.** Risks and assumptions (each with how serious it is), the starting dashboard, who is invited and when, and a summary. You sign off, then start the project.

A step is **Complete** when nothing is left to answer on it. The list on the right shows everything still to answer across all steps; click one to go to it. If something does not apply to your project, say so ("Not used for this project", with a reason if you wish).

## Signing off and starting

You sign off on what is on the page. If you change anything afterwards, the sign-off is stale and you sign off again. Starting the project creates it with everything you set up, makes it active and sends the invitations you chose. If it stops partway, the project stays a draft and **Try again** carries on from where it stopped; nothing is created twice.

## What is not done for you

Some things are not set up by the wizard because they follow from other parts of the product:

- **Who verifies values and who approves reports or spending** follows each person's role on the project.
- **The checklist** is built from your data and the donor's requirements as each report is prepared.
- **How confidential evidence is** is chosen for each file when it is added.
- **A donor's own report layout** is uploaded after the project has started.
- **Budget lines that pay for the whole project** belong to no single activity; add them on the Finance page.
- **Targets by group, formulas and donor indicator mapping** are set on the Logframe page.

The report you see after starting lists anything that could not be done, with the reason.
