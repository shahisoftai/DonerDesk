# DonorDesk — One-Page Concept Note for Approval

## 1. Project Title

**DonorDesk: AI-Powered Donor Reporting and Evidence Compliance Platform**

## 2. Concept Summary

DonorDesk is a web-based platform designed for NGOs, INGOs, humanitarian programmes, and donor-funded projects. It helps teams convert scattered field updates, logframes, indicator data, activity evidence, photos, attendance sheets, procurement files, and donor templates into structured donor-ready reports and audit-ready evidence packs.

The core promise is simple:

**From messy field evidence to donor-ready reports and compliance-ready documentation.**

## 3. Problem

NGO and humanitarian reporting is still heavily manual. Teams often rely on Excel, Word, WhatsApp, email, Google Drive, Kobo exports, and scattered folders to prepare donor reports.

This creates major pain points:

Reports take too long to prepare.  
Evidence is scattered and difficult to verify.  
Indicator achievements are not always linked to proof.  
Donor templates are manually reformatted every cycle.  
Missing annexes are discovered too late.  
Audit preparation becomes stressful and reactive.  
Institutional knowledge is lost when staff change.

As a result, organizations face delayed submissions, weak evidence trails, donor dissatisfaction, and compliance risk.

## 4. Proposed Solution

DonorDesk will provide a single workspace where teams can manage donor reporting, evidence mapping, indicator updates, and compliance readiness.

The MVP will allow users to:

Create donor-funded project workspaces.  
Upload donor reporting templates.  
Upload or enter logframes and indicators.  
Upload field evidence and supporting documents.  
Submit activity updates.  
Map evidence to activities, outputs, indicators, and donor requirements.  
Generate AI-assisted donor report drafts.  
Identify missing evidence and compliance gaps.  
Export final reports and evidence checklists.

The system will not replace human review. It will assist teams by organizing evidence, drafting reports, flagging gaps, and improving reporting quality.

## 5. Target Users

DonorDesk is intended for:

Local NGOs  
National NGOs  
Small and mid-sized INGOs  
UN implementing partners  
Humanitarian consultants  
Donor-funded programme units  
M&E teams  
Grants and reporting teams  
Compliance and audit teams  
Programme managers  
Field officers

Initial sectors can include nutrition, food security, health, WASH, education, protection, livelihoods, and emergency response.

## 6. MVP Features

The initial MVP will include:

Organization account and user roles  
Project setup  
Donor template upload and extraction  
Logframe and indicator manager  
Evidence library  
Manual and AI-assisted evidence tagging  
Activity update form  
Reporting period setup  
AI-assisted report draft generation  
Missing evidence and compliance checklist  
Review comments  
Report readiness score  
PDF/Word report export  
Evidence checklist export  
Basic audit log

The MVP will stay focused on donor reporting and evidence compliance only. It will not include full accounting, procurement, HR, CRM, or ERP features.

## 7. AI Capabilities

AI will be used for practical workflow support, including:

Extracting donor template sections  
Structuring pasted logframes  
Classifying uploaded evidence  
Suggesting links between evidence, activities, and indicators  
Converting rough field notes into donor-style paragraphs  
Drafting report sections from verified sources  
Flagging unsupported claims  
Identifying missing evidence and compliance gaps

All AI outputs will remain editable and subject to human approval. The platform will emphasize source-linked reporting, traceability, and auditability.

## 8. Key Differentiation

Generic AI tools can write text, but they cannot reliably prove whether a donor report is supported by the right evidence.

DonorDesk will differentiate by connecting:

Narrative reports  
Logframes  
Indicators  
Activity updates  
Evidence files  
Donor requirements  
Compliance checklists  
Audit trails

This makes DonorDesk more than an AI writing tool. It becomes a reporting and evidence governance workspace for donor-funded projects.

Additional revenue can come from:

Paid pilot setup  
Donor template configuration  
Report preparation support  
Training packages  
Custom dashboards  
Data migration  
Compliance review services

## 10. Validation Plan

Before full-scale development, DonorDesk should be validated through:

Interviews with 15–20 NGO, M&E, grants, and compliance professionals  
Collection of 5–10 real donor reporting templates  
Clickable prototype testing  
One manual pilot using real or sample project data  
Feedback on pricing, usability, and report quality  
Conversion of early users into paid pilots
