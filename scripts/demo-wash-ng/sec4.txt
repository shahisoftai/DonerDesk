The project responded to the floods in Jere and Konduga LGAs with three main actions while the July cholera alert remained in force. Teams rehabilitated three water points after the floods contaminated them, disinfected and retested the affected water points, and raised latrine access paths for the households that had lost access.
The project released a flood contingency of hygiene kits and 1,100 households received kits. Teams conducted 55 post-flood water quality tests; chlorine compliance dropped from 74 to 66 percent after the floods contaminated three water points, and the water points were disinfected and retested in response. A water point committee chair in Jere chlorinated the flooded borehole with the project test kit.
